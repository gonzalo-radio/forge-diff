✏️Melee Shrouding3.6.0 beta: When a unit where most models have this rule is targeted by an enemy, as long as this unit hasn't started an activation yet this round, attackers get -2" range to a min. of 6" when charging it.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule is targeted by an enemy unit for charging, that enemy unit gets -2" range to a min. of 6".
✏️Precision Spotter3.6.0 beta: Once per activation, pick one enemy unit within 30" and in line of sight of this model and roll one die, on a 4+ place a token on it. Friendly units may remove tokens from their target before rolling to hit to get +X to hit rolls when attacking, where X is the number of removed tokens.
