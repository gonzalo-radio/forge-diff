✏️Caster
core rule		3.5.1: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t hold more than 6 tokens at once. At any point before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Roll one die, on 4+ resolve the effect on a target in line of sight. Models within 18” in line of sight of the caster’s unit may spend any number of spell tokens at the same time before rolling, to give the caster +1/-1 to the roll per token.
3.6.0: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t have more than 6 at once. Once per activation, before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Pick a target and roll one die, and on 4+ resolve the spell’s effect. Models within 18” of the Caster may spend any number of spell tokens at the same time before rolling to give the Caster +1/-1 to the roll per token.
✏️Fortified3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
3.6.0: When units where most models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
✏️Highborn3.5.1: Moves +2" when using Advance, and +2” when using Rush/Charge.
3.6.0: This model gets +6" range when using Hold actions and shooting, and moves +2" when using Charge actions.
✏️Hit & Run3.5.1: Once per round, units where all models have this rule may move by up to 3" after shooting or being in melee.
3.6.0: Once per round, this model may move by up to 3" after shooting or being in melee.
✏️Hit & Run Shooter3.5.1: Once per round, units where all models have this rule may move by up to 3" after shooting.
3.6.0: Once per round, this model may move by up to 3" after shooting.
✏️Piercing Spotter3.5.1: Once per activation, pick one enemy unit within 30" and in line of sight of this model and roll one die, on a 4+ place a marker on it. When attacking, friendly units may remove markers from their target before rolling to block to get +AP(X) on their weapons, where X is the number of removed markers.
3.6.0: Once per activation, pick one enemy unit within 30" and in line of sight of this model and roll one die, on a 4+ place a token on it. When attacking, friendly units may remove tokens from their target before rolling to block to get +AP(X) on their weapons, where X is the number of removed tokens.
✏️Regeneration
core rule		3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Scout
core rule		3.5.1: May be set aside before deployment. After all other units are deployed, may be deployed anywhere fully within 12” of their deployment zone. Players alternate in placing Scout units, starting with the player that activates next.
3.6.0: May be set aside before deployment. After all other units are deployed, may be deployed anywhere fully within 9” of their deployment zone, over 9” away from non-Scout enemy units. Players alternate in placing Scout units, starting with the player that activates next.
✏️Shred
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
✏️Stealth
core rule		3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: When units where most models have this rule are shot from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
