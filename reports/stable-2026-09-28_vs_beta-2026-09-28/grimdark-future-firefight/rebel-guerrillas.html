Special rules

NameText
➕Enhanced GuerrillaIf most models in this unit have Guerrilla, they may move by up to 6" from Guerrilla (instead of only 3").
➕Enhanced Guerrilla AuraThis model's unit and up to 2 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Enhanced Guerrilla. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
➕ReapWhen attacking units that are Shaken or Fatigued, the target must re-roll successful unmodified Defense results of 5-6.
➕VanguardAfter this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9" of its position, over 9" away from non-Vanguard enemy units.
➖Counter-AttackStrikes first when charged.
➖Guerrilla BoostIf most models in this unit have Guerrilla, they may move by up to 6" from Guerrilla (instead of only 3").
➖Guerrilla Boost AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Guerrilla Boost. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
➖LacerateWhen attacking the target must re-roll unmodified Defense results of 6.
✏️Bane when Shooting Aura3.5.1: This model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Bane when shooting. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
3.6.0: This model's unit and up to 2 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Bane when shooting. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
✏️Furious
core rule		3.5.1: When charging, unmodified results of 6 to hit in melee deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: When charging, unmodified result of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Guerrilla3.5.1: Once per round, units where all models have this rule may move by up to 3" after shooting or being in melee.
3.6.0: Once per round, this model may move by up to 3" after shooting or being in melee.
✏️Hit & Run3.5.1: Once per round, units where all models have this rule may move by up to 3" after shooting or being in melee.
