Special rules

NameText
➕Enhanced ViolentIf this model has Violent, enemies always take wounds on unmodified results of 1 to block from Violent (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕Enhanced WarboundIf this model has Warbound, enemies always take wounds on unmodified results of 1 to block from Warbound (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕PurgeWhen attacking units that are Shaken or Fatigued, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➕Spell ResistanceWhen a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➕Unique HeroThis hero may only be taken once per party.
➖+1 to DefenseThis model gets +1 to defense rolls.
➖Bounding AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Bounding. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖CrackOn unmodified results of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+2).
➖Reckless Piercing AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Reckless Piercing. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖ResistanceWhen a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➖Storm of WarOnly one use per game. Once per game, when this model is activated, before attacking, roll 3 dice. For each 2+ one enemy unit within 12" takes 3 hits with AP(1).
➖Warbound BoostIf this model has Warbound, when it shoots or charges enemies over 9" away, enemies taking wounds from it take extra wounds on failed defense rolls of 1-2 from Warbound (instead of only on 1).
✏️Blast
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X. Hits must be split evenly between the target and all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how), but each unit may only take up to as many hits as models in it.
3.6.0: Ignores cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X, where X is up to as many hits as enemy models in the target unit and within 3” of it. Hits must be split evenly between all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how).
✏️Bounding3.5.1: When this unit is activated, you may place all models with this rule in it anywhere fully within D3+1" of their position.
3.6.0: When this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3+1".
✏️Fatigue Debuff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which must take a morale test. If failed, it becomes fatigued.
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which must take a Morale Test. If failed, it becomes Fatigued.
✏️Melee Evasion3.5.1: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where most models have this rule.
✏️Piercing Fighter Aura3.5.1: This model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get AP(+1) in melee. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
3.6.0: This model's unit and up to 2 friendly units picked before the start of the game get AP(+1) in melee. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
✏️Ravage3.5.1: When it's this model's turn to attack in melee, roll X dice. For each 6+ the target takes one wound.
3.6.0: Once per round, when it’s this model’s turn to strike in melee, roll X dice before attacking, unless this unit is Fatigued. For each 6+ the target takes one wound.
✏️Reckless Piercing3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule is activated, you may roll one die. On a 2+ their weapons get AP(+1) when attacking until the end of the round, but on a 1 enemy units get AP(+1) on their weapons when attacking them instead.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule is activated, you may roll one die. On a 2+ their weapons get AP(+1) when attacking until the end of the round, but on a 1 enemy units get AP(+1) on their weapons when attacking them instead.
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Shielded3.5.1: Units where all models have this rule get +1 to defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.
3.6.0: Units where most models have this rule get +1 to Defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.
✏️Violent3.5.1: Enemies that roll to block hits from this model's weapons take 1 extra wound for each unmodified result of 1 that they roll.
3.6.0: When this model attacks enemies for the first time each round, unmodified results of 1 to block hits from weapons deal 1 extra wound.
✏️Warbound3.5.1: Enemies that roll to block hits from this model's weapons take 1 extra wound for each unmodified result of 1 that they roll.
3.6.0: When this model attacks enemies for the first time each round, unmodified results of 1 to block hits from weapons deal 1 extra wound.
