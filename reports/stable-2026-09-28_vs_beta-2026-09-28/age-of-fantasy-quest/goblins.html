Special rules

NameText
➕Enhanced MischievousIf this model has Mischievous, enemies taking hits from it must re-roll successful unmodified Defense results of 5-6 from Mischievous (instead of only results of 6).
➕Enhanced ViciousIf this model has Vicious, enemies taking hits from it must re-roll successful unmodified Defense results of 5-6 from Vicious (instead of only results of 6).
➕HazardousGets AP(4), but on unmodified results of 1 to hit this model takes one wound, or X wounds if this weapon has the Blast(X)/Deadly(X) rule.
➕Heavy ImpactCounts as having Impact(X) with hits that have AP(1).
➖Mischievous BoostIf this model has Mischievous, when it shoots or charges enemies over 9" away, enemies taking hits from it must re-roll successful unmodified defense results of 5-6 from Mischievous (instead of only on 6).
➖ShredOn unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
➖TearAgainst non-Hero units with Tough(3) or more, this weapon gets AP(+4).
✏️Bounding3.5.1: When this unit is activated, you may place all models with this rule in it anywhere fully within D3+1" of their position.
3.6.0: When this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3+1".
✏️Melee Evasion3.5.1: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where most models have this rule.
✏️Piercing Fighting Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets AP(+1) in melee against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets AP(+1) in melee against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Precision Fighter Buff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 friendly units within 12", which gets +1 to hit rolls in melee once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 friendly units within 12", which gets +1 to hit rolls in melee once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Takedown
core rule		3.5.1: This model may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target, which is resolved as if it was a unit of [1]. Takedown attacks must be resolved before other weapons.
3.6.0: This model may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target for this weapon's attacks, which is resolved as if it was a unit of [1]. Takedown attacks must be resolved before other weapons.
✏️Takedown Strike3.5.1: Once per game, when it's this model's turn to attack in melee, it may make one attack at Quality 2+ with AP(2), Deadly(3), and Takedown.
3.6.0: Once per game, when it's this model's turn to strike in melee, it may make X attacks at Quality 2+ with AP(2), Deadly(3), and Takedown.
