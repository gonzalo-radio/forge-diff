Special rules

NameText
➕Enhanced GuardedIf most models in this unit have Guarded, they always get +1 to Defense rolls from Guarded (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕Enhanced SturdyIf most models in this unit have Sturdy, they always get +1 to Defense rolls from Sturdy (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕Precision ChargeThis model gets +1 to hit rolls when charging.
➕Quick ThrowThis model may always target enemies that are not in line of sight when shooting with Indirect weapons (instead of only when using Hold actions).
➕ScourgeFor each unmodified result of 1 to block hits, this weapon counts as having dealt 2 extra wounds when checking who won melee, or if shooting wounds would trigger Morale Tests.
➕Spell AccumulatorGets X accumulator tokens at the start of each round, but can’t hold more than 6 tokens at once. Casters from other friendly units within 12" may spend this model's accumulator tokens as if they were their own spell tokens.
➕Spell ResistanceWhen a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➕Unique HeroThis hero may only be taken once per party.
➕UnwieldyStrikes last when charging.
➕VanguardAfter this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9" of its position, over 9" away from non-Vanguard enemy units.
➖Precision Charge AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get +1 to hit rolls when charging. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖ResistanceWhen a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➖ShredOn unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
➖Sturdy BoostIf all models in this unit have Sturdy, they always get +1 to defense rolls from Sturdy (instead of only when being shot/charged from over 9" away).
✏️Blast
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X. Hits must be split evenly between the target and all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how), but each unit may only take up to as many hits as models in it.
3.6.0: Ignores cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X, where X is up to as many hits as enemy models in the target unit and within 3” of it. Hits must be split evenly between all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how).
✏️Fortified3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
3.6.0: When units where most models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
✏️Guarded3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot or charged from over 9" away, they get +1 to defense rolls.
3.6.0: If most models in this unit have this rule, the first time they are attacked each round, they get +1 to Defense rolls.
✏️Ravage3.5.1: When it's this model's turn to attack in melee, roll X dice. For each 6+ the target takes one wound.
3.6.0: Once per round, when it’s this model’s turn to strike in melee, roll X dice before attacking, unless this unit is Fatigued. For each 6+ the target takes one wound.
✏️Rending
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Regeneration, and on unmodified results of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
3.6.0: Ignores Regeneration, and for each unmodified result of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Speed Debuff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which moves -2" when using Advance actions and -4" when using Rush/Charge actions once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Once per activation, before attacking, pick one enemy model within 18" in line of sight, which moves -2" when using Advance actions and -4" when using Rush/Charge actions once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Sturdy3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot or charged from over 9" away, they get +1 to defense rolls.
3.6.0: If most models in this unit have this rule, the first time they are attacked each round, they get +1 to Defense rolls.
