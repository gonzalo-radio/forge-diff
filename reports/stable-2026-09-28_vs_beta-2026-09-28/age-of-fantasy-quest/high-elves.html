Special rules

NameText
➕BoundingWhen this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3+1".
➕Enhanced Extra ReachIf this model has Extra Reach, it gets +12" range when using Hold actions and shooting, and moves +4" when using Charge actions from Extra Reach (instead of only +6" and +2").
➕Enhanced HighbornIf this model has Highborn, it gets +12" range when using Hold actions and shooting, and moves +4" when using Charge actions from Highborn (instead of only +6" and +2").
➕Extra ReachThis model gets +6" range when using Hold actions and shooting, and moves +2" when using Charge actions.
➕FractureFor each unmodified result of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+2).
➕Hit & RunOnce per round, this model may move by up to 3" after shooting or being in melee.
➕SlayerWhen shooting enemies over 9" away, or when charging, this model's weapons get AP(+2) if most models in the target have Tough(27) or higher.
➕Spell ResistanceWhen a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➕VanguardAfter this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9" of its position, over 9" away from non-Vanguard enemy units.
➖Breath AttackOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, roll one die. On a 2+ one enemy unit within 6" in line of sight takes 1 hit with Blast(3) and AP(1).
➖CrackOn unmodified results of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+2).
➖Highborn BoostIf this model has Highborn, it moves +4" on Advance and +4" on Rush/Charge from Highborn (instead of only +2" and +2").
➖Precision DebuffOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to hit rolls when attacking once (next time the effect would apply).
➖QuickMoves +2" when using Advance, and +2” when using Rush/Charge.
➖Quick ShotThis model may shoot after using Rush actions.
➖ResistanceWhen a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➖StrafingOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
✏️Blast
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X. Hits must be split evenly between the target and all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how), but each unit may only take up to as many hits as models in it.
3.6.0: Ignores cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X, where X is up to as many hits as enemy models in the target unit and within 3” of it. Hits must be split evenly between all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how).
✏️Caster
core rule		3.5.1: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t hold more than 6 tokens at once. At any point before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Roll one die, on 4+ resolve the effect on a target in line of sight. Heroes may spend any number of spell tokens before rolling to get +1 to the roll per token, or to give an enemy caster within 18” and in line of sight -1 to their roll per token. Whenever a hero fails to cast a spell, it may spend X*2 power to count as having cast it instead, where X is the spell’s casting value.
3.6.0: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t have more than 6 at once. Once per activation, before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Pick a target and roll one die, and on 4+ resolve the spell’s effect. Heroes may spend any number of tokens/power before rolling to get +1 to the roll per token/power, or to give an enemy Caster within 18” -1 to their roll per token/power.
✏️Highborn3.5.1: Moves +2" when using Advance, and +2” when using Rush/Charge.
3.6.0: This model gets +6" range when using Hold actions and shooting, and moves +2" when using Charge actions.
✏️Piercing Tag3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, pick one enemy unit within 24" and in line of sight of it, and place X markers on it. When attacking, friendly units may remove markers from their target before rolling to block to get +AP(Y) on their weapons, where Y is the number of removed markers.
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, place X tokens on one enemy unit within 24" and in line of sight, or one token on X enemy units within 24" and in line of sight. When attacking, friendly units may remove one token from their target before rolling to block to get +AP(+1) on their weapons.
✏️Precision Shooting Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units get +1 to hit rolls when shooting against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units get +1 to hit rolls when shooting against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Shielded3.5.1: Units where all models have this rule get +1 to defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.
3.6.0: Units where most models have this rule get +1 to Defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.
✏️Shred
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
