NameText
➕Crossing StrikeOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
➕Enhanced Versatile WarriorIf this model has Versatile Attack, it always gets either AP(+1) or +1 to hit rolls from Versatile Attack (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕Enhanced Vinci TechIf this model has Versatile Attack, it always gets either AP(+1) or +1 to hit rolls from Versatile Attack (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕Piercing SpotterOnly one use per game. Once per activation, pick one enemy unit within 30" and in line of sight of this model and roll one die, on a 4+ place a token on it. When attacking, friendly units may remove tokens from their target before rolling to block to get +AP(X) on their weapons, where X is the number of removed tokens.
➕PunctureIgnores Regeneration, and when attacking units where most models have Defense 2+ to Defense 3+, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➕Quick ThrowThis model may always target enemies that are not in line of sight when shooting with Indirect weapons (instead of only when using Hold actions).
➕RavageOnce per round, when it’s this model’s turn to strike in melee, roll X dice before attacking, unless this unit is Fatigued. For each 6+ the target takes one wound.
➕Unique HeroThis hero may only be taken once per party.
➕Versatile WarriorWhen this unit is activated, pick one effect: until the end of the activation, when this unit attacks enemies for the first time each round, all models with this rule in it either get AP(+1) or get +1 to hit rolls.
➖DisintegrateIgnores Regeneration, and against units where most models have Defense 2+ to Defense 3+, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➖Precision Shooting MarkOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units get +1 to hit rolls when shooting against once (next time the effect would apply).
➖ShieldedUnits where all models have this rule get +1 to defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.
➖StrafingOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
➖SurgeOn unmodified results of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hits (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
➖UnpredictableWhen attacking, roll one die and apply one effect to all models with this rule: on a 1-3 they get AP(+1), and on a 4-6 they get +1 to hit rolls instead.
➖Unpredictable Shooter AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Unpredictable Shooter. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖Versatile AttackWhen this unit is activated, pick one effect: until the end of the activation, when shooting or charging enemies over 9" away, all models with this rule in this unit either get AP(+1) or get +1 to hit rolls.
➖Vinci Tech BoostIf this model has Vinci Tech, it always gets both AP(+1) and +1 to hit rolls from Vinci Tech (instead of having to pick between AP(+1) or +1 to hit rolls).
✏️Fortified3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
3.6.0: When units where most models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
✏️Impact
core rule		3.5.1: Roll X dice when attacking after charging, unless fatigued. For each 2+ the target takes one hit.
3.6.0: When charging, before either unit attacks, roll X dice, unless this unit is Fatigued. For each 2+ the target takes 1 hit.
✏️Increased Shooting Range Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units get +6" range when shooting against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units get +6" range when shooting against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Precision Spotter3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, pick one enemy unit within 30" and in line of sight of this model and roll one die, on a 4+ place a marker on it. Friendly units may remove markers from their target before rolling to hit to get +X to hit rolls when attacking, where X is the number of removed markers.
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, pick one enemy unit within 30" and in line of sight of this model and roll one die, on a 4+ place a token on it. Friendly units may remove tokens from their target before rolling to hit to get +X to hit rolls when attacking, where X is the number of removed tokens.
✏️Protected3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored.
✏️Ranged Slayer3.5.1: When this model shoots at enemies over 9" away, its weapons get AP(+2) if most models in the target have Tough(3) or higher.
3.6.0: When shooting at enemies over 9" away, this model's weapons get AP(+2) if most models in the target have Tough(27) or higher.
✏️Regeneration
core rule		3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Shred
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
✏️Steadfast3.5.1: If a unit where all models have this rule is Shaken at the beginning of the round, roll one die. On a 4+, it stops being Shaken.
3.6.0: If a unit where most models have this rule is Shaken at the beginning of the round, roll one die. On a 4+, it stops being Shaken.
✏️Takedown
core rule		3.5.1: This model may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target, which is resolved as if it was a unit of [1]. Takedown attacks must be resolved before other weapons.
3.6.0: This model may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target for this weapon's attacks, which is resolved as if it was a unit of [1]. Takedown attacks must be resolved before other weapons.
✏️Takedown Strike3.5.1: Once per game, when it's this model's turn to attack in melee, it may make one attack at Quality 2+ with AP(2), Deadly(3), and Takedown.
3.6.0: Once per game, when it's this model's turn to strike in melee, it may make X attacks at Quality 2+ with AP(2), Deadly(3), and Takedown.
✏️Unstoppable
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Regeneration, and ignores all negative modifiers to this weapon.
3.6.0: Ignores Regeneration, and ignores all negative modifiers to this weapon which are not from this model's special rules.
✏️Vinci Tech3.5.1: When this unit is activated, pick one effect: until the end of the activation, when shooting or charging enemies over 9" away, all models with this rule in this unit either get AP(+1) or get +1 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: When this unit is activated, pick one effect: until the end of the activation, when this unit attacks enemies for the first time each round, all models with this rule in it either get AP(+1) or get +1 to hit rolls.
