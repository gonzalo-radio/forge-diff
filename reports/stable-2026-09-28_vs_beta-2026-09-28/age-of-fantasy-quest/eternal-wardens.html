Special rules

NameText
➕BoundingWhen this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3+1".
➕Enhanced FortifiedIf most models in this unit have Fortified, hits they take count as having AP(-2) from Fortified (instead of only AP(-1)).
➕Enhanced WardenIf most models in this unit have Warden, hits they take count as having AP(-2) from Warden (instead of only AP(-1)).
➕FortifiedWhen units where most models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
➕Heavy ImpactCounts as having Impact(X) with hits that have AP(1).
➕SmashWhen attacking units over half of this weapon's range away, the target must re-roll unmodified Defense results of 6.
➕UnwieldyStrikes last when charging.
➖Breath AttackOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, roll one die. On a 2+ one enemy unit within 6" in line of sight takes 1 hit with Blast(3) and AP(1).
➖LacerateWhen attacking the target must re-roll unmodified Defense results of 6.
➖ReinforcedWhen units where all models have this rule are shot or charged from over 9" away and take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
➖Warden BoostIf all models in this unit have Warden, enemy hits always count as having AP(-1) from Warden (instead of only when being shot/charged from over 9" away).
✏️Piercing Target3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, pick one enemy unit within 18" and in line of sight of it, and place X markers on it. Friendly units get +AP(X) on their weapons when attacking it.
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, pick X enemy units within 18" and in line of sight of it, and place one token on them. Friendly units get AP(+1) on their weapons when attacking them.
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Unstoppable
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Regeneration, and ignores all negative modifiers to this weapon.
3.6.0: Ignores Regeneration, and ignores all negative modifiers to this weapon which are not from this model's special rules.
✏️Unstoppable Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Unstoppable against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Unstoppable against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Vanguard3.5.1: After this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9” of its position.
3.6.0: After this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9" of its position, over 9" away from non-Vanguard enemy units.
✏️Warden3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot or charged from over 9" away and take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
3.6.0: When units where most models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
