Special rules

NameText
➕Enhanced SteadfastIf most models in this unit have Steadfast, they stops being Shaken on rolls of 3+ from Steadfast (instead of only on 4+).
➕Enhanced Vale OathIf most models in this unit have Vale Oath, they stops being Shaken on rolls of 3+ from Vale Oath (instead of only on 4+).
➕ObliterateIgnores Regeneration, and when attacking units where most models have Tough(27) or higher, the target must re-roll successful unmodified Defense results of 5-6.
➕Spell ResistanceWhen a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➕Versatile WarriorWhen this unit is activated, pick one effect: until the end of the activation, when this unit attacks enemies for the first time each round, all models with this rule in it either get AP(+1) or get +1 to hit rolls.
➖Piercing ShooterThis model gets AP(+1) when shooting.
➖ShredOn unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
➖TearAgainst non-Hero units with Tough(3) or more, this weapon gets AP(+4).
➖Vale Oath BoostIf this model has Vale Oath, it stops being Shaken on rolls of 3+ from Vale Oath (instead of only on 4+).
➖Versatile AttackWhen this unit is activated, pick one effect: until the end of the activation, when shooting or charging enemies over 9" away, all models with this rule in this unit either get AP(+1) or get +1 to hit rolls.
✏️Fortified3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
3.6.0: When units where most models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
✏️Impact
core rule		3.5.1: Roll X dice when attacking after charging, unless fatigued. For each 2+ the target takes one hit.
3.6.0: When charging, before either unit attacks, roll X dice, unless this unit is Fatigued. For each 2+ the target takes 1 hit.
✏️Melee Shrouding3.5.1: Enemies get -3" movement to a min. of 6" when trying to charge units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule is targeted by an enemy, as long as this unit hasn't started an activation yet this round, attackers get -2" range to a min. of 6" when charging it.
✏️Ranged Slayer3.5.1: When this model shoots at enemies over 9" away, its weapons get AP(+2) if most models in the target have Tough(3) or higher.
3.6.0: When shooting at enemies over 9" away, this model's weapons get AP(+2) if most models in the target have Tough(27) or higher.
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Steadfast3.5.1: If a unit where all models have this rule is Shaken at the beginning of the round, roll one die. On a 4+, it stops being Shaken.
3.6.0: If a unit where most models have this rule is Shaken at the beginning of the round, roll one die. On a 4+, it stops being Shaken.
✏️Surge
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hits (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Surge Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Surge when attacking against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Surge when attacking against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Vale Oath3.5.1: If a unit where all models have this rule is Shaken at the beginning of the round, roll one die. On a 4+, it stops being Shaken.
3.6.0: If a unit where most models have this rule is Shaken at the beginning of the round, roll one die. On a 4+, it stops being Shaken.
✏️Vanguard3.5.1: After this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9” of its position.
3.6.0: After this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9" of its position, over 9" away from non-Vanguard enemy units.
