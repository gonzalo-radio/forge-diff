Special rules

NameText
➕Enhanced FortifiedIf most models in this unit have Fortified, hits they take count as having AP(-2) from Fortified (instead of only AP(-1)).
➕Enhanced OssifiedIf most models in this unit have Ossified, hits they take count as having AP(-2) from Ossified (instead of only AP(-1)).
➕FortifiedWhen units where most models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
➕ShatterFor each unmodified result of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+3).
➖Breath AttackOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, roll one die. On a 2+ one enemy unit within 6" in line of sight takes 1 hit with Blast(3) and AP(1).
➖DestructiveOn unmodified results of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
➖Ossified BoostIf all models in this unit have Ossified, enemy hits always count as having AP(-1) from Ossified (instead of only when being shot/charged from over 9" away).
➖Piercing ShooterThis model gets AP(+1) when shooting.
➖PreciseGets +1 to hit when attacking.
➖ReinforcedWhen units where all models have this rule are shot or charged from over 9" away and take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
✏️Caster
core rule		3.5.1: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t hold more than 6 tokens at once. At any point before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Roll one die, on 4+ resolve the effect on a target in line of sight. Heroes may spend any number of spell tokens before rolling to get +1 to the roll per token, or to give an enemy caster within 18” and in line of sight -1 to their roll per token. Whenever a hero fails to cast a spell, it may spend X*2 power to count as having cast it instead, where X is the spell’s casting value.
3.6.0: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t have more than 6 at once. Once per activation, before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Pick a target and roll one die, and on 4+ resolve the spell’s effect. Heroes may spend any number of tokens/power before rolling to get +1 to the roll per token/power, or to give an enemy Caster within 18” -1 to their roll per token/power.
✏️Evasive3.5.1: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls when attacking units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls when attacking units where most models have this rule.
✏️Evasive Aura3.5.1: This model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Evasive. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
3.6.0: This model's unit and up to 2 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Evasive. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
✏️Furious
core rule		3.5.1: When charging, unmodified results of 6 to hit in melee deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: When charging, unmodified result of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Furious Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Furious against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Furious against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Ossified3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot or charged from over 9" away and take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
3.6.0: When units where most models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
✏️Precision Feat3.5.1: Once per game, when this unit attacks and all its models have this rule, you may use this rule so that they get +1 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: Once per game, when this unit attacks, you may use this rule so that all models with this rule in it get +1 to hit rolls.
✏️Regeneration
core rule		3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Regeneration Buff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 friendly units within 12", which gets Regeneration once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 friendly units within 12", which gets Regeneration once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Unstoppable
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Regeneration, and ignores all negative modifiers to this weapon.
3.6.0: Ignores Regeneration, and ignores all negative modifiers to this weapon which are not from this model's special rules.
