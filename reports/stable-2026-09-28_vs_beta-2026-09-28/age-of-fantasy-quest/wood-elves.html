Special rules

NameText
➕DodgeIf most models in this unit have this rule, as long as they aren't Fatigued, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls in melee against them.
➕Enhanced ShroudedIf most models in this unit have Shrouded, attackers always get -4"/-2" range when targeting it from Shrouded (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕Enhanced Wild VeilIf most models in this unit have Wild Veil, attackers always get -4"/-2" range when targeting it from Wild Veil (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕Grounded DefenseIf a unit where most models have this rule has most of them within 1" of terrain, they get +1 to Defense rolls.
➕Heavy ImpactCounts as having Impact(X) with hits that have AP(1).
➕Piercing ChargeThis model gets AP(+1) when charging.
➕SmashWhen attacking units over half of this weapon's range away, the target must re-roll unmodified Defense results of 6.
➕Unique HeroThis hero may only be taken once per party.
➖Breath AttackOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, roll one die. On a 2+ one enemy unit within 6" in line of sight takes 1 hit with Blast(3) and AP(1).
➖DestructiveOn unmodified results of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
➖Grounded Protection AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Grounded Protection. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖Grounded ReinforcementIf a unit where all models have this rule has most of them within 1" of terrain, they get +1 to defense rolls.
➖Piercing AssaultThis model gets AP(+1) when charging.
➖ResistanceWhen a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➖ShredOn unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
➖Wild Veil BoostIf all models in this unit have Wild Veil, enemies get -8" range to a min. of 6" when shooting them, and -4" movement to a min. of 6" when charging them from Wild Veil (instead of only -4" and -2").

3.6.0: If a unit where most models have this rule has most of them within 1" of terrain, when they take wounds roll one die per wound, and on a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Hit & Run Shooter3.5.1: Once per round, units where all models have this rule may move by up to 3" after shooting.
3.6.0: Once per round, this model may move by up to 3" after shooting.
✏️Melee Evasion3.5.1: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where most models have this rule.
✏️Quick Shot Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Quick Shot against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Quick Shot against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Shrouded3.5.1: Enemies get -4" range to a min. of 6" when trying to shoot at units where all models have this rule, and -2" movement to a min. of 6" when trying to charge units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule is targeted by an enemy, as long as this unit hasn't started an activation yet this round, attackers get -4"/-2" range to a min. of 6" when shooting/charging it.
