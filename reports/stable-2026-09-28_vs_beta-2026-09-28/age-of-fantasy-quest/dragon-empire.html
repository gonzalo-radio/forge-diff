Special rules

NameText
➕Crossing StrikeOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
➕DemolishIgnores Cover, and when attacking units with most models within 1" of terrain, this weapon gets AP(+1).
➕Enhanced PredatorIf this model has Predator, it gets extra attacks on successful unmodified hit results of 5-6 from Predator (instead of only on 6).
➕Enhanced Royal WarriorIf this model has Royal Warrior, it gets extra attacks on successful unmodified hit results of 5-6 from Royal Warrior (instead of only on 6).
➕Spell ResistanceWhen a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➖Breath AttackOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, roll one die. On a 2+ one enemy unit within 6" in line of sight takes 1 hit with Blast(3) and AP(1).
➖ResistanceWhen a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➖Royal Warrior BoostIf this model has Royal Warrior, it gets extra attacks on successful unmodified hit results of 5-6 from Royal Warrior (instead of only on 6).
➖StrafingOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
➖TearAgainst non-Hero units with Tough(3) or more, this weapon gets AP(+4).
✏️Indirect Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Indirect against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Indirect against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Precision Debuff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to hit rolls when attacking once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to hit rolls when attacking once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Precision Fighter Buff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 friendly units within 12", which gets +1 to hit rolls in melee once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 friendly units within 12", which gets +1 to hit rolls in melee once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Shielded3.5.1: Units where all models have this rule get +1 to defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.
3.6.0: Units where most models have this rule get +1 to Defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.
✏️Surge
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hits (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
