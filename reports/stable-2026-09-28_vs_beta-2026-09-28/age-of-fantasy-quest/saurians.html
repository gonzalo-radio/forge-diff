Special rules

NameText
➕Crossing StrikeOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
➕Enhanced PredatorIf this model has Predator, it gets extra attacks on successful unmodified hit results of 5-6 from Predator (instead of only on 6).
➕Enhanced PrimalIf this model has Primal, it gets extra attacks on successful unmodified hit results of 5-6 from Primal (instead of only on 6).
➕Enhanced Primal BuffOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 friendly units within 12", which gets Enhanced Primal once (next time the effect would apply).
➕PreciseGets +1 to hit when attacking.
➕PunctureIgnores Regeneration, and when attacking units where most models have Defense 2+ to Defense 3+, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➕Spell ConduitCasters within 12" that are from other friendly units may cast spells as if they were in this model's position, and get +1 to casting rolls when doing so. Friendly casters may only use this rule if this unit isn't Shaken.
➕Unique HeroThis hero may only be taken once per party.
➕Unpredictable FighterWhen in melee, roll one die and apply one effect to all models with this rule: on a 1-3 they get AP(+1), and on a 4-6 they get +1 to hit rolls instead.
➕VanguardAfter this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9" of its position, over 9" away from non-Vanguard enemy units.
➕Versatile WarriorWhen this unit is activated, pick one effect: until the end of the activation, when this unit attacks enemies for the first time each round, all models with this rule in it either get AP(+1) or get +1 to hit rolls.
➖Counter-AttackStrikes first when charged.
➖DisintegrateIgnores Regeneration, and against units where most models have Defense 2+ to Defense 3+, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➖FortifiedWhen units where all models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
➖Primal BoostIf this model has Primal, it gets extra attacks on successful unmodified hit results of 5-6 from Primal (instead of only on 6).
➖Primal Boost BuffOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 friendly units within 12", which gets Primal Boost once (next time the effect would apply).
➖RavageWhen it's this model's turn to attack in melee, roll X dice. For each 6+ the target takes one wound.
➖Regeneration AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Regeneration. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖StrafingOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
➖SurgeOn unmodified results of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hits (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
➖Takedown when ShootingThis model gets Takedown when shooting.
✏️Bounding3.5.1: When this unit is activated, you may place all models with this rule in it anywhere fully within D3+1" of their position.
3.6.0: When this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3+1".
✏️Evasive3.5.1: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls when attacking units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls when attacking units where most models have this rule.
✏️Hit & Run Shooter3.5.1: Once per round, units where all models have this rule may move by up to 3" after shooting.
3.6.0: Once per round, this model may move by up to 3" after shooting.
✏️Impact
core rule		3.5.1: Roll X dice when attacking after charging, unless fatigued. For each 2+ the target takes one hit.
3.6.0: When charging, before either unit attacks, roll X dice, unless this unit is Fatigued. For each 2+ the target takes 1 hit.
✏️Precision Spotter3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, pick one enemy unit within 30" and in line of sight of this model and roll one die, on a 4+ place a marker on it. Friendly units may remove markers from their target before rolling to hit to get +X to hit rolls when attacking, where X is the number of removed markers.
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, pick one enemy unit within 30" and in line of sight of this model and roll one die, on a 4+ place a token on it. Friendly units may remove tokens from their target before rolling to hit to get +X to hit rolls when attacking, where X is the number of removed tokens.
✏️Precision Target3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, pick one enemy unit within 18" and in line of sight of it, and place X markers on it. Friendly units get +X to hit rolls when attacking it.
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, pick X enemy units within 18" and in line of sight of it, and place one token on them. Friendly units get +1 to hit rolls when attacking them.
✏️Protection Feat3.5.1: Once per game, when this unit takes wounds and all its models have this rule, you may use this rule and roll one die per wound, and on a 5+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: Once per game, when this unit takes wounds and most of its models have this rule, you may use this rule and roll one die per wound, and on a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Regeneration
core rule		3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Rending
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Regeneration, and on unmodified results of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
3.6.0: Ignores Regeneration, and for each unmodified result of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Shred
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
✏️Shred Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Shred when attacking against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Shred when attacking against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Steadfast3.5.1: If a unit where all models have this rule is Shaken at the beginning of the round, roll one die. On a 4+, it stops being Shaken.
3.6.0: If a unit where most models have this rule is Shaken at the beginning of the round, roll one die. On a 4+, it stops being Shaken.
