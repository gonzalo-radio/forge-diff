Special rules

NameText
➕DisintegrateIgnores Regeneration, and when attacking units where most models have Tough(27) or higher, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➕Enhanced BestialIf this model has Bestial, enemies taking hits from it must re-roll successful unmodified Defense results of 5-6 from Bestial (instead of only results of 6).
➕Enhanced ViciousIf this model has Vicious, enemies taking hits from it must re-roll successful unmodified Defense results of 5-6 from Vicious (instead of only results of 6).
➕Piercing ChargeThis model gets AP(+1) when charging.
➕Spell DrainOnce per activation, before attacking, pick one enemy model within 18" in line of sight, which must take a Morale Test. If failed, it loses 2 spell tokens.
➕Spell ResistanceWhen a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➕Unique HeroThis hero may only be taken once per party.
➖Bestial BoostIf this model has Bestial, when it shoots or charges enemies over 9" away, enemies taking hits from it must re-roll successful unmodified defense results of 5-6 from Bestial (instead of only on 6).
➖Casting DebuffOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight with Caster, which gets -1 to casting rolls once (next time the effect would apply).
➖Furious AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Furious. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖Piercing AssaultThis model gets AP(+1) when charging.
➖ShredOn unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
➖TearAgainst non-Hero units with Tough(3) or more, this weapon gets AP(+4).
✏️Melee Evasion3.5.1: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where most models have this rule.
✏️Mind Control3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which must take a morale test. If failed you may move it by up to 6" in a straight line in any direction.
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which must take a Morale Test. If failed you may move it by up to 6" in a straight line in any direction. Units may only be moved once per round in this way.
✏️Morale Debuff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to morale test rolls once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to Morale Test rolls once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Precision Shooter Aura3.5.1: This model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get +1 to hit rolls when shooting. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
3.6.0: This model's unit and up to 2 friendly units picked before the start of the game get +1 to hit rolls when shooting. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
✏️Precision Tag3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, pick one enemy unit within 24" and in line of sight of it, and place X markers on it. When attacking, friendly units may remove markers from their target before rolling to hit to get +Y to hit rolls, where Y is the number of removed markers.
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, place X tokens on one enemy unit within 24" and in line of sight, or one token on X enemy units within 24" and in line of sight. When attacking, friendly units may remove one token from their target before rolling to hit to get +1 to hit rolls.
✏️Ravage3.5.1: When it's this model's turn to attack in melee, roll X dice. For each 6+ the target takes one wound.
3.6.0: Once per round, when it’s this model’s turn to strike in melee, roll X dice before attacking, unless this unit is Fatigued. For each 6+ the target takes one wound.
✏️Regeneration
core rule		3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Surge
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hits (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
