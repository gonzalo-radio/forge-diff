Special rules

NameText
➕Ambush Re-DeploymentOnce per game, when a unit where all models have this rule ends its activation, you may immediately remove it from the table (dropping any objectives it might hold within 1"), and deploy it as if it had Ambush at the beginning of the next round.
➕DemolishIgnores Cover, and when attacking units with most models within 1" of terrain, this weapon gets AP(+1).
➕Enhanced Gloom-MistIf most models in this unit have Gloom-Mist, enemies attacking them always get -1 to hit rolls from Gloom-Mist (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕Enhanced ScreenedIf most models in this unit have Screened, enemies attacking them always get -1 to hit rolls from Screened (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕Unpredictable FighterWhen in melee, roll one die and apply one effect to all models with this rule: on a 1-3 they get AP(+1), and on a 4-6 they get +1 to hit rolls instead.
➖Counter-AttackStrikes first when charged.
➖DisintegrateIgnores Regeneration, and against units where most models have Defense 2+ to Defense 3+, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➖Gloom-Mist BoostIf all models in this unit have Gloom-Mist, enemies attacking them always get -1 to hit rolls from Gloom-Mist (instead of only when being shot/charged from over 9" away).
➖Versatile AttackWhen this unit is activated, pick one effect: until the end of the activation, when shooting or charging enemies over 9" away, all models with this rule in this unit either get AP(+1) or get +1 to hit rolls.
➖Versatile Attack AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Versatile Attack. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
✏️Gloom-Mist3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot or charged from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: If most models in this unit have this rule, the first time they are attacked each round, enemy units attacking them get -1 to hit rolls.
✏️Melee Shrouding3.5.1: Enemies get -3" movement to a min. of 6" when trying to charge units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule is targeted by an enemy, as long as this unit hasn't started an activation yet this round, attackers get -2" range to a min. of 6" when charging it.
✏️Rending
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Regeneration, and on unmodified results of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
3.6.0: Ignores Regeneration, and for each unmodified result of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
✏️Screened3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot or charged from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: If most models in this unit have this rule, the first time they are attacked each round, enemy units attacking them get -1 to hit rolls.
✏️Slayer3.5.1: When this model shoots at enemies over 9" away, or when it charges, its weapons get AP(+2) if most models in the target have Tough(3) or higher.
3.6.0: When shooting enemies over 9" away, or when charging, this model's weapons get AP(+2) if most models in the target have Tough(27) or higher.
✏️Slayer Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Slayer against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Slayer against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Takedown
core rule		3.5.1: This model may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target, which is resolved as if it was a unit of [1]. Takedown attacks must be resolved before other weapons.
3.6.0: This model may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target for this weapon's attacks, which is resolved as if it was a unit of [1]. Takedown attacks must be resolved before other weapons.
✏️Takedown Strike3.5.1: Once per game, when it's this model's turn to attack in melee, it may make one attack at Quality 2+ with AP(2), Deadly(3), and Takedown.
3.6.0: Once per game, when it's this model's turn to strike in melee, it may make X attacks at Quality 2+ with AP(2), Deadly(3), and Takedown.
