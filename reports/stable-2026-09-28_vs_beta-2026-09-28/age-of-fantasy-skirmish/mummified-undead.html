✏️Blast
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X, where X is up to as many hits as enemy models in the target unit and within 3” of it. Hits must be split evenly between all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (defender picks how).
3.6.0: Ignores Cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X. Hits must be split evenly between the target and all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (defender picks how), but each unit may only take up to as many hits as models in it
✏️Fortified3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
3.6.0: When units where most models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
✏️Fortified Aura3.5.1: This model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Fortified. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
