Special rules

NameText
➕Crossing StrikeOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
➕DeathstrikeIf this model is killed in melee, the attacking unit takes X hits.
➕DecimateWhen attacking units with more than half of their total size or Tough value (for single-model units), the target must re-roll unmodified Defense results of 6.
➕Enhanced InfectedIf this model has Infected, enemies always take wounds on unmodified results of 1 to block from Infected (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕Enhanced ViolentIf this model has Violent, enemies always take wounds on unmodified results of 1 to block from Violent (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕Hit & Run ShooterOnce per round, this model may move by up to 3" after shooting.
➕Regeneration BuffOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 friendly units within 12", which gets Regeneration once (next time the effect would apply).
➕SpawnAdd a friendly AI unit of X to the party, which is deployed as if it were a hero. This unit is activated after all Heroes, and follows the AI activation rules.
➕Unpredictable FighterWhen in melee, roll one die and apply one effect to all models with this rule: on a 1-3 they get AP(+1), and on a 4-6 they get +1 to hit rolls instead.
➕VanguardAfter this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9" of its position, over 9" away from non-Vanguard enemy units.
➖Infected BoostIf this model has Infected, when it shoots or charges enemies over 9" away, enemies taking wounds from it take extra wounds on failed defense rolls of 1-2 from Infected (instead of only on 1).
➖LacerateWhen attacking the target must re-roll unmodified Defense results of 6.
✏️Blast
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X. Hits must be split evenly between the target and all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how), but each unit may only take up to as many hits as models in it.
3.6.0: Ignores cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X, where X is up to as many hits as enemy models in the target unit and within 3” of it. Hits must be split evenly between all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how).
✏️Fortified3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
3.6.0: When units where most models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
✏️Infected3.5.1: Enemies that roll to block hits from this model's weapons take 1 extra wound for each unmodified result of 1 that they roll.
3.6.0: When this model attacks enemies for the first time each round, unmodified results of 1 to block hits from weapons deal 1 extra wound.
✏️Precision Debuff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to hit rolls when attacking once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to hit rolls when attacking once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Regeneration
core rule		3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Surge
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hits (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Violent3.5.1: Enemies that roll to block hits from this model's weapons take 1 extra wound for each unmodified result of 1 that they roll.
3.6.0: When this model attacks enemies for the first time each round, unmodified results of 1 to block hits from weapons deal 1 extra wound.
