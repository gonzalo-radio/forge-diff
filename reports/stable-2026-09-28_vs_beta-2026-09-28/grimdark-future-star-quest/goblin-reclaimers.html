➕Enhanced ViciousIf this model has Vicious, enemies taking hits from it must re-roll successful unmodified Defense results of 5-6 from Vicious (instead of only results of 6).
➕HazardousGets AP(4), but on unmodified results of 1 to hit this model takes one wound, or X wounds if this weapon has the Blast(X)/Deadly(X) rule.
➕ImpreciseGets -1 to hit when attacking.
➕Precision FighterThis model gets +1 to hit rolls in melee.
➕Unique HeroThis hero may only be taken once per party.
➖Courage AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get +1 to morale test rolls. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖Mischievous BoostIf this model has Mischievous, when it shoots or charges enemies over 9" away, enemies taking hits from it must re-roll successful unmodified defense results of 5-6 from Mischievous (instead of only on 6).
➖Precision Fighter AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get +1 to hit rolls in melee. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖ShredOn unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
➖TearAgainst non-Hero units with Tough(3) or more, this weapon gets AP(+4).
✏️Blast
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X. Hits must be split evenly between the target and all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how), but each unit may only take up to as many hits as models in it.
3.6.0: Ignores cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X, where X is up to as many hits as enemy models in the target unit and within 3” of it. Hits must be split evenly between all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how).
✏️Bounding3.5.1: When this unit is activated, you may place all models with this rule in it anywhere fully within D3+1" of their position.
3.6.0: When this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3+1".
✏️Dangerous Terrain Debuff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which counts as being in Dangerous Terrain once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which counts as being in Dangerous terrain once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Melee Evasion3.5.1: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where most models have this rule.
✏️Regeneration
core rule		3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Regeneration Aura3.5.1: This model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Regeneration. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
3.6.0: This model's unit and up to 2 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Regeneration. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
✏️Surge
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hits (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Unpredictable Shooter Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Unpredictable Shooter against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Unpredictable Shooter against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Vanguard3.5.1: After this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9” of its position.
3.6.0: After this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9" of its position, over 9" away from non-Vanguard enemy units.
