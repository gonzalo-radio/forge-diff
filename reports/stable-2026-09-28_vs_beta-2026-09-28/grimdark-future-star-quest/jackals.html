Special rules

NameText
➕DisintegrateIgnores Regeneration, and when attacking units where most models have Tough(27) or higher, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➕Enhanced ScrapperIf this model has Scrapper, enemies taking hits from it must re-roll successful unmodified Defense results of 5-6 from Scrapper (instead of only results of 6).
➕Enhanced ViciousIf this model has Vicious, enemies taking hits from it must re-roll successful unmodified Defense results of 5-6 from Vicious (instead of only results of 6).
➕Heavy ImpactCounts as having Impact(X) with hits that have AP(1).
➕Precision FighterThis model gets +1 to hit rolls in melee.
➕Unique HeroThis hero may only be taken once per party.
➖DestructiveOn unmodified results of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
➖FortifiedWhen units where all models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
➖Melee EvasionEnemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where all models have this rule.
➖Precision Fighter AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get +1 to hit rolls in melee. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖Scrapper BoostIf this model has Scrapper, when it shoots or charges enemies over 9" away, enemies taking hits from it must re-roll successful unmodified defense results of 5-6 from Scrapper (instead of only on 6).
➖ShredOn unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
➖Stealth AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Stealth. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖StrafingOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
➖SurgeOn unmodified results of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hits (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Bounding3.5.1: When this unit is activated, you may place all models with this rule in it anywhere fully within D3+1" of their position.
3.6.0: When this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3+1".
✏️Mind Control3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which must take a morale test. If failed you may move it by up to 6" in a straight line in any direction.
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which must take a Morale Test. If failed you may move it by up to 6" in a straight line in any direction. Units may only be moved once per round in this way.
✏️Morale Debuff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to morale test rolls once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to Morale Test rolls once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Precision Tag3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, pick one enemy unit within 24" and in line of sight of it, and place X markers on it. When attacking, friendly units may remove markers from their target before rolling to hit to get +Y to hit rolls, where Y is the number of removed markers.
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, place X tokens on one enemy unit within 24" and in line of sight, or one token on X enemy units within 24" and in line of sight. When attacking, friendly units may remove one token from their target before rolling to hit to get +1 to hit rolls.
✏️Ranged Shrouding3.5.1: Enemies get -6" range to a min. of 6" when trying to shoot units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule is targeted by an enemy, as long as this unit hasn't started an activation yet this round, attackers get -4"" range to a min. of 6" when shooting at it.
✏️Regeneration
core rule		3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Shatter3.5.1: Against non-Hero units with Tough(3) or more, this weapon gets AP(+2).
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+3).
✏️Shielded3.5.1: Units where all models have this rule get +1 to defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.
3.6.0: Units where most models have this rule get +1 to Defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.
