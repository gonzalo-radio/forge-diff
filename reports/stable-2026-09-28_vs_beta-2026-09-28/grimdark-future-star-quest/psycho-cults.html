Special rules

NameText
➕Enhanced PredatorIf this model has Predator, it gets extra attacks on successful unmodified hit results of 5-6 from Predator (instead of only on 6).
➕Enhanced PsychoticIf this model has Psychotic, it gets extra attacks on successful unmodified hit results of 5-6 from Psychotic (instead of only on 6).
➕PreciseGets +1 to hit when attacking.
➕PunctureIgnores Regeneration, and when attacking units where most models have Defense 2+ to Defense 3+, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➖DisintegrateIgnores Regeneration, and against units where most models have Defense 2+ to Defense 3+, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➖Psychotic BoostIf this model has Psychotic, it gets extra attacks on successful unmodified hit results of 5-6 from Psychotic (instead of only on 6).
✏️Bane Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Bane when attacking against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Bane when attacking against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Bounding3.5.1: When this unit is activated, you may place all models with this rule in it anywhere fully within D3+1" of their position.
3.6.0: When this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3+1".
✏️Protection Feat3.5.1: Once per game, when this unit takes wounds and all its models have this rule, you may use this rule and roll one die per wound, and on a 5+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: Once per game, when this unit takes wounds and most of its models have this rule, you may use this rule and roll one die per wound, and on a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Protection Feat Aura3.5.1: This model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Protection Feat. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
3.6.0: This model's unit and up to 2 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Protection Feat. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
✏️Regeneration
core rule		3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Shielded3.5.1: Units where all models have this rule get +1 to defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.
3.6.0: Units where most models have this rule get +1 to Defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.
✏️Surge
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hits (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
