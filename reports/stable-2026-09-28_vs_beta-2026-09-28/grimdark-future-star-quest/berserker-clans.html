Special rules

NameText
➕Enhanced BerserkerIf this model has Berserker, the target always takes hits on unmodified hit results of 6 from Berserker (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕Enhanced BrutalIf this model has Brutal, the target always takes hits on unmodified hit results of 6 from Brutal (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕InstinctiveWhen this model is activated, if it is able to shoot/charge an enemy unit, then it must immediately attack the closest valid target and gets +1 to hit rolls for that attack.
➕PierceIgnores Cover, and when attacking units over half of this weapon's range away, this weapon gets AP(+1).
➕Precision FighterThis model gets +1 to hit rolls in melee.
➖Berserker BoostIf this model has Berserker, when it shoots or charges enemies over 9" away, it deals extra hits on successful unmodified hit results of 5-6 from Berserker (instead of only on 6).
➖Precision Fighter AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get +1 to hit rolls in melee. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖Speed FeatOnce per game, when this unit moves and all its models have this rule, you may use this rule so that they move +2" when using Advance actions and +4" when using Rush/Charge actions.
➖Speed Feat AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Speed Feat. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖TearAgainst non-Hero units with Tough(3) or more, this weapon gets AP(+4).
✏️Berserker3.5.1: When attacking, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: When this model attacks enemies for the first time each round, unmodified result of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Blast
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X. Hits must be split evenly between the target and all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how), but each unit may only take up to as many hits as models in it.
3.6.0: Ignores cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X, where X is up to as many hits as enemy models in the target unit and within 3” of it. Hits must be split evenly between all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how).
✏️Brutal3.5.1: When attacking, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: When this model attacks enemies for the first time each round, unmodified result of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Melee Evasion3.5.1: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where most models have this rule.
✏️Piercing Fighting Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets AP(+1) in melee against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets AP(+1) in melee against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Surge
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hits (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
