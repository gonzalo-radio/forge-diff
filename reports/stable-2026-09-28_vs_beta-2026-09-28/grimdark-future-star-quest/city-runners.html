Special rules

NameText
➕AgileMoves +1" when using Advance, and +2" when using Rush/Charge.
➕Enhanced AgileIf this model has Agile, it moves +2" on Advance and +4" on Rush/Charge from Agile (instead of only +1" and +2").
➕Enhanced RunnerIf this model has Runner, it moves +2" on Advance and +4" on Rush/Charge from Runner (instead of only +1" and +2").
➕FractureFor each unmodified result of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+2).
➕Precision TagOnly one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, place X tokens on one enemy unit within 24" and in line of sight, or one token on X enemy units within 24" and in line of sight. When attacking, friendly units may remove one token from their target before rolling to hit to get +1 to hit rolls.
➖CrackOn unmodified results of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+2).
➖Piercing TagOnly one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, pick one enemy unit within 24" and in line of sight of it, and place X markers on it. When attacking, friendly units may remove markers from their target before rolling to block to get +AP(Y) on their weapons, where Y is the number of removed markers.
➖QuickMoves +2" when using Advance, and +2” when using Rush/Charge.
➖Runner BoostIf this model has Runner, it moves +4" on Advance and +4" on Rush/Charge from Runner (instead of only +2" and +2").
➖SurgeOn unmodified results of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hits (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Blast
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X. Hits must be split evenly between the target and all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how), but each unit may only take up to as many hits as models in it.
3.6.0: Ignores cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X, where X is up to as many hits as enemy models in the target unit and within 3” of it. Hits must be split evenly between all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how).
✏️Hit & Run Fighter3.5.1: Once per round, units where all models have this rule may move by up to 3" after being in melee.
3.6.0: Once per round, this model may move by up to 3" after being in melee.
✏️Melee Shrouding3.5.1: Enemies get -3" movement to a min. of 6" when trying to charge units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule is targeted by an enemy, as long as this unit hasn't started an activation yet this round, attackers get -2" range to a min. of 6" when charging it.
✏️Rapid Charge Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Rapid Charge against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Rapid Charge against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Regeneration
core rule		3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Runner3.5.1: Moves +2" when using Advance, and +2” when using Rush/Charge.
3.6.0: Moves +1" when using Advance, and +2" when using Rush/Charge.
