Special rules

NameText
➕Ambushing Piercing ShotCounts as having Ambush, and its weapons get AP(+1) when shooting on the round in which it deploys via this rule.
➕DestructiveWhen attacking units with more than half of their total size or Tough value (for single-model units), those hits get AP(+1).
➕Enhanced NimbleIf this model has Nimble, it may move by up to 2D3" from Nimble (instead of only D3").
➕Enhanced Rapid BlinkIf this model has Rapid Blink, it may move by up to 2D3" from Rapid Blink (instead of only D3").
➕Increased Shooting RangeThis model gets +6" range when shooting.
➕InfiltrateCounts as having Ambush, but may be deployed anywhere over 3" away from enemy unit.
➕Infiltrating Precision StrikeCounts as having Infiltrate, and gets +1 to hit rolls in melee on the round in which it deploys via this rule.
➕Repel AmbushersEnemy units using Ambush must be set up over 12" away from this model's unit.
➕Takedown ShotOnce per game, when this model shoots, it may make X extra attacks against the target at Quality 2+ with AP(2), Deadly(3), and Takedown.
➕WardingWhen a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, the first time they are attacked each round, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➖Counter-AttackStrikes first when charged.
➖DisintegrateIgnores Regeneration, and against units where most models have Defense 2+ to Defense 3+, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➖EvasiveEnemies get -1 to hit rolls when attacking units where all models have this rule.
➖Point-Blank SurgeWhen this model shoots at enemies within 12", unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
➖Rapid Blink BoostIf this model has Rapid Blink, it may be placed within 2D3" from Rapid Blink (instead of only within D3").
➖ShredOn unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
✏️Nimble3.5.1: When this unit is activated, you may place all models with this rule in it anywhere fully within D3" of their position.
3.6.0: When this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3".
✏️Rapid Blink3.5.1: When this unit is activated, you may place all models with this rule in it anywhere fully within D3" of their position.
3.6.0: When this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3".
✏️Shielded3.5.1: Units where all models have this rule get +1 to defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.
3.6.0: Units where most models have this rule get +1 to Defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.
✏️Slayer3.5.1: When this model shoots at enemies over 9" away, or when it charges, its weapons get AP(+2) if most models in the target have Tough(3) or higher.
3.6.0: When shooting enemies over 9" away, or when charging, this model's weapons get AP(+2) if most models in the target have Tough(27) or higher.
✏️Slayer Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Slayer against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Slayer against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Takedown
core rule		3.5.1: This model may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target, which is resolved as if it was a unit of [1]. Takedown attacks must be resolved before other weapons.
3.6.0: This model may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target for this weapon's attacks, which is resolved as if it was a unit of [1]. Takedown attacks must be resolved before other weapons.
✏️Takedown Strike3.5.1: Once per game, when it's this model's turn to attack in melee, it may make one attack at Quality 2+ with AP(2), Deadly(3), and Takedown.
3.6.0: Once per game, when it's this model's turn to strike in melee, it may make X attacks at Quality 2+ with AP(2), Deadly(3), and Takedown.
