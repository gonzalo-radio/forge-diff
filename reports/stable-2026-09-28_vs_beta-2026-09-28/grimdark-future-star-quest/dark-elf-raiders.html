Special rules

NameText
➕BraveryUnits where most models have this rule get +1 to Morale Test rolls.
➕DodgeIf most models in this unit have this rule, as long as they aren't Fatigued, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls in melee against them.
➕Enhanced HarassingIf most models in this unit have Harassing, they may move by up to 6" from Harassing (instead of only 3").
➕Enhanced Hit & RunIf most models in this unit have Hit & Run, they may move by up to 6" from Hit & Run (instead of only 3").
➕Heavy ImpactCounts as having Impact(X) with hits that have AP(1).
➕Melee SlayerThis model's melee weapons get AP(+2) against units where most models have Tough(9) or higher.
➕Piercing MarksmanWhen shooting at enemies over 9" away, this model's weapons get AP(+1).
➖Courage AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get +1 to morale test rolls. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖Harassing BoostIf most models in this unit have Harassing, they may move by up to 6" from Harassing (instead of only 3").
➖Melee EvasionEnemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where all models have this rule.
➖Piercing HunterWhen this model shoots at enemies over 9" away, its weapons get AP(+1).
✏️Harassing3.5.1: Once per round, units where all models have this rule may move by up to 3" after shooting or being in melee.
3.6.0: Once per round, this model may move by up to 3" after shooting or being in melee.
✏️Hit & Run3.5.1: Once per round, units where all models have this rule may move by up to 3" after shooting or being in melee.
3.6.0: Once per round, this model may move by up to 3" after shooting or being in melee.
✏️Melee Shrouding3.5.1: Enemies get -3" movement to a min. of 6" when trying to charge units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule is targeted by an enemy, as long as this unit hasn't started an activation yet this round, attackers get -2" range to a min. of 6" when charging it.
✏️Precision Fighting Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units get +1 to hit rolls in melee against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units get +1 to hit rolls in melee against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Regeneration
core rule		3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Regeneration Buff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 friendly units within 12", which gets Regeneration once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 friendly units within 12", which gets Regeneration once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Regenerative Strength3.5.1: Place one marker on this model when it ignores a wound. When in melee, pick one of its weapons to get +X attacks, where X is the number of markers on it.
3.6.0: Place one token on this model when it ignores a wound. Once per round, when it’s this model’s turn to strike in melee, roll as many dice as tokens on it before attacking, unless this unit is Fatigued. For each 4+ the target takes one wound.
✏️Stealth
core rule		3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: When units where most models have this rule are shot from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
✏️Vanguard3.5.1: After this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9” of its position.
3.6.0: After this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9" of its position, over 9" away from non-Vanguard enemy units.
