Special rules

NameText
➕DemolishIgnores Cover, and when attacking units with most models within 1" of terrain, this weapon gets AP(+1).
➕Grounded DefenseIf a unit where most models have this rule has most of them within 1" of terrain, they get +1 to Defense rolls.
➕Grounded Defense AuraThis model's unit and up to 2 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Grounded Defense. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
➕Piercing MarksmanWhen shooting at enemies over 9" away, this model's weapons get AP(+1).
➕Unpredictable FighterWhen in melee, roll one die and apply one effect to all models with this rule: on a 1-3 they get AP(+1), and on a 4-6 they get +1 to hit rolls instead.
➖DestructiveOn unmodified results of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
➖Grounded ReinforcementIf a unit where all models have this rule has most of them within 1" of terrain, they get +1 to defense rolls.
➖Grounded Reinforcement AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Grounded Reinforcement. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖Piercing HunterWhen this model shoots at enemies over 9" away, its weapons get AP(+1).
➖ReinforcementWhen a unit where all models have this rule is Shaken or fully destroyed, you may remove it from the table as destroyed and place a new copy of it fully within 12" of any table edge at the beginning of the next round after Ambushers have been deployed. Units that deploy via Reinforcement can’t seize or contest objectives on the round they deploy, and this rule doesn’t apply to the new copy of the unit.
➖ShieldedUnits where all models have this rule get +1 to defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.
✏️Grounded Precision3.5.1: If a unit where all models have this rule has most of them within 1" of terrain, they get +1 to hit rolls when attacking.
3.6.0: If most models in this unit are within 1" of terrain, this model gets +1 to hit rolls when attacking.
✏️Slayer3.5.1: When this model shoots at enemies over 9" away, or when it charges, its weapons get AP(+2) if most models in the target have Tough(3) or higher.
3.6.0: When shooting enemies over 9" away, or when charging, this model's weapons get AP(+2) if most models in the target have Tough(27) or higher.
✏️Slayer Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Slayer against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Slayer against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Strider
core rule		3.5.1: May ignore the effects of difficult terrain when moving, and only needs 2+ to pass jumping rolls.
3.6.0: May ignore the effects of Difficult terrain. This unit halves the number of dice it rolls when jumping (rounding up).
✏️Strider Aura3.5.1: This model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Strider. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
3.6.0: This model's unit and up to 2 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Strider. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
✏️Union Assault3.5.1: After this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9” of its position.
3.6.0: After this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9" of its position, over 9" away from non-Vanguard enemy units.
✏️Vanguard3.5.1: After this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9” of its position.
3.6.0: After this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9" of its position, over 9" away from non-Vanguard enemy units.
