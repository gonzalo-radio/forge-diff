Special rules

NameText
➕Ambush Re-DeploymentOnce per game, when a unit where all models have this rule ends its activation, you may immediately remove it from the table (dropping any objectives it might hold within 1"), and deploy it as if it had Ambush at the beginning of the next round.
➕DemolishIgnores Cover, and when attacking units with most models within 1" of terrain, this weapon gets AP(+1).
➕Enhanced Gloom-MistIf most models in this unit have Gloom-Mist, enemies attacking them always get -1 to hit rolls from Gloom-Mist (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕Enhanced Gloom-Mist AuraThis model's unit and up to 2 nearest friendly units picked before the start of the game get Enhanced Gloom-Mist. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
➕Enhanced ScreenedIf most models in this unit have Screened, enemies attacking them always get -1 to hit rolls from Screened (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕Grounded EvasionIf a unit where most models have this rule has most of them within 1" of terrain, enemies get -1 to hit rolls when attacking them.
➕UnpredictableWhen attacking, roll one die and apply one effect to all models with this rule: on a 1-3 they get AP(+1), and on a 4-6 they get +1 to hit rolls instead.
➕Unpredictable Fighter AuraThis model's unit and up to 2 nearest friendly units picked before the start of the game get Unpredictable Fighter. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
➖Counter-AttackStrikes first when charged.
➖DisintegrateIgnores Regeneration, and against units where most models have Defense 2+ to Defense 3+, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➖Gloom-Mist BoostIf all models in this unit have Gloom-Mist, enemies attacking them always get -1 to hit rolls from Gloom-Mist (instead of only when being shot/charged from over 9" away).
➖Gloom-Mist Boost AuraThis model and up to 3 nearest friendly units picked before the start of the game get Gloom-Mist Boost. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
➖Grounded StealthIf a unit where all models have this rule has most of them within 1" of terrain, enemies get -1 to hit rolls when attacking them.
➖Versatile AttackWhen this unit is activated, pick one effect: until the end of the activation, when shooting or charging enemies over 9" away, all models with this rule in this unit either get AP(+1) or get +1 to hit rolls.
➖Versatile Attack AuraThis model and up to 3 nearest friendly units picked before the start of the game get Versatile Attack. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
✏️Ambush
core rule		3.5.1: Only deploys D3 rounds afters its wave is deployed, and at the start of the round it's placed at the centre of a random table quarter.
3.6.0: Only deploys D3 rounds afters its wave, and at the start of the round it's placed within 6” of a random AI Goal.
✏️Gloom-Mist3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot or charged from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: If most models in this unit have this rule, the first time they are attacked each round, enemy units attacking them get -1 to hit rolls.
✏️Melee Shrouding3.5.1: Enemies get -3" movement to a min. of 6" when trying to charge units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule is targeted by an enemy, as long as this unit hasn't started an activation yet this round, attackers get -2" range to a min. of 6" when charging it.
✏️Melee Shrouding Aura3.5.1: This model and up to 3 nearest friendly units picked before the start of the game get Melee Shrouding. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
3.6.0: This model's unit and up to 2 nearest friendly units picked before the start of the game get Melee Shrouding. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
✏️Rending
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Regeneration, and on unmodified results of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
