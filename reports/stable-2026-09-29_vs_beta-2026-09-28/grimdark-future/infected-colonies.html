➕Crossing StrikeOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
➕DecimateWhen attacking units with more than half of their total size or Tough value (for single-model units), the target must re-roll unmodified Defense results of 6.
➕Enhanced InfectedIf this model has Infected, enemies always take wounds on unmodified results of 1 to block from Infected (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕Enhanced Infected AuraThis model and its unit get Enhanced Infected.
➕Enhanced ViolentIf this model has Violent, enemies always take wounds on unmodified results of 1 to block from Violent (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕Hit & Run ShooterOnce per round, this model may move by up to 3" after shooting.
➕Regeneration BuffOnce per activation, before attacking, pick one friendly unit within 12", which gets Regeneration once (next time the effect would apply).
➕SpawnOnce per game, when this model is activated, you may place a new unit of X fully within 6" of it.
➕Unpredictable FighterWhen in melee, roll one die and apply one effect to all models with this rule: on a 1-3 they get AP(+1), and on a 4-6 they get +1 to hit rolls instead.
➕VanguardAfter this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9" of its position, over 9" away from non-Vanguard enemy units.
➖Infected BoostIf this model has Infected, when it shoots or charges enemies over 9" away, enemies taking wounds from it take extra wounds on failed defense rolls of 1-2 from Infected (instead of only on 1).

➖LacerateWhen attacking the target must re-roll unmodified Defense results of 6.

✏️Precision Growth3.5.1: Place one marker on this unit at the beginning of each round if it's on the table and isn't Shaken, up to a max. of four markers. For every two markers, models with this rule in this unit get +1 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: Place one token on this unit at the beginning of each round if it's on the table and isn't Shaken, up to a max. of four tokens. For every two tokens, models with this rule in this unit get +1 to hit rolls.

core rule		3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
