Grimdark Future core rules — changes 3.5.1 → 3.6.0

Common rules of Grimdark Future (/api/rules/common).

Core rules

NameText
➕AssaultCounts as both a ranged weapon and a melee weapon.
➕BraceEnemies charging this unit get -X total Impact rolls, unless this unit is Fatigued.
➕ParryWhen this unit takes wounds in melee whilst not Fatigued, roll X dice per model with this rule in it, up to as many dice as wounds taken. For each 6+ one wound is ignored.
➕ShredFor each unmodified result of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
➕Spell AmplifierGets +1 to casting rolls.
➕SurgeFor each unmodified result of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
➖EntrenchedAs long as a unit where all models have this rule hasn't moved during the round, enemy units shooting at it from over 9" away get -2 to hit rolls.
➖MoraleGets +X to morale test rolls.
➖Sniper REMOVEShoots at Quality 2+, and each model with Sniper may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target, which is resolved as if it was a unit of 1. Sniper shooting must be resolved before other weapons.
✏️Aircraft3.5.1: May only use Advance actions, moving in a straight line, and adding 30” to its total move (even if Shaken). Aircraft ignore all units and terrain when moving and stopping, can’t seize or contest objectives, can’t be charged, and units targeting them get -12” range.
3.6.0: May only use Advance actions, moving in a straight line, and adding 30” to its total move (even if Shaken). Aircraft ignore all units and terrain when moving and stopping, can’t seize or contest objectives, can’t be charged, and units targeting them get -12” range and -1 to hit rolls.
✏️Artillery3.5.1: May only use Hold actions. When this model shoots at enemies over 9" away, it gets +1 to hit rolls. When enemy units shoot at this model from over 9" away, they get -2 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: When this model uses Hold actions and shoots at enemies over 12” away, it gets +1 to hit rolls
✏️Banner3.5.1: This model and its unit get +1 to morale test rolls.
3.6.0: This model and its unit get +1 to Morale Test rolls triggered by shooting/melee.
✏️Caster3.5.1: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t hold more than 6 tokens at once. At any point before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Roll one die, on 4+ resolve the effect on a target in line of sight. Models within 18” in line of sight of the caster’s unit may spend any number of spell tokens at the same time before rolling, to give the caster +1/-1 to the roll per token.
3.6.0: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t have more than 6 at once. Once per activation, before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Pick a target and roll one die, and on 4+ resolve the spell’s effect. Models within 18” of the Caster may spend any number of spell tokens at the same time before rolling to give the Caster +1/-1 to the roll per token.
✏️Counter3.5.1: Strikes first with this weapon when charged, and the charging unit gets -1 total Impact rolls per model with Counter.
3.6.0: May strike first with this weapon when charged, unless this unit is Fatigued.
✏️Fearless3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule fails a morale test, roll one die. On a 4+ it counts as passed instead.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule fails a Morale Test, roll one die. On a 4+ it counts as passed instead.
✏️Flying3.5.1: May move through units and terrain, and ignores terrain effects whilst moving.
3.6.0: May move through units and terrain, and ignores the effects of terrain it doesn't end its move in.
✏️Furious3.5.1: When charging, unmodified results of 6 to hit in melee deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: When charging, unmodified result of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Hero3.5.1: Heroes with up to Tough(6) may deploy as part of one multi-model unit without another Hero. The hero may take morale tests on behalf of the unit, but must use the unit’s Defense until all other models have been killed.
3.6.0: Heroes with up to Tough(6) may deploy as part of one multi-model unit without another Hero, but must use the unit’s Defense until all other models have been killed. The Hero’s unit, and units within 6” of friendly Heroes with Tough(9) or higher, may use the Hero’s Quality for Morale Tests as long as the Hero isn’t Shaken.
✏️Impact3.5.1: Roll X dice when attacking after charging, unless fatigued. For each 2+ the target takes one hit.
3.6.0: When charging, before either unit attacks, roll X dice, unless this unit is Fatigued. For each 2+ the target takes 1 hit.
✏️Indirect3.5.1: Gets -1 to hit rolls when shooting after moving. May target enemies that are not in line of sight as if in line of sight, and ignores cover from sight obstructions.
3.6.0: When using Hold actions and shooting, may target enemies that are not in line of sight as if in line of sight, and ignores Cover from Sight Blockers.
✏️Regeneration3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Relentless3.5.1: When this model shoots at enemies over 9" away, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: When this model shoots at enemies over 9” away, unmodified result of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Rending3.5.1: Ignores Regeneration, and on unmodified results of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
3.6.0: Ignores Regeneration, and for each unmodified result of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
✏️Scout3.5.1: May be set aside before deployment. After all other units are deployed, may be deployed anywhere fully within 12” of their deployment zone. Players alternate in placing Scout units, starting with the player that activates next.
3.6.0: May be set aside before deployment. After all other units are deployed, may be deployed anywhere fully within 9” of their deployment zone, over 9” away from non-Scout enemy units. Players alternate in placing Scout units, starting with the player that activates next.
✏️Stealth3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: When units where most models have this rule are shot from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
✏️Strider3.5.1: May ignore the effects of difficult terrain when moving.
3.6.0: May ignore the effects of Difficult terrain.
✏️Takedown3.5.1: This model may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target, which is resolved as if it was a unit of [1]. Takedown attacks must be resolved before other weapons.
3.6.0: This model may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target for this weapon's attacks, which is resolved as if it was a unit of [1]. Takedown attacks must be resolved before other weapons.
✏️Transport3.5.1: May transport up to X models or Heroes with up to Tough(6), and non-Heroes with up to Tough(3) which occupy 3 spaces each. Transports may deploy with units inside, and units may enter/exit by using any move action, but must stay fully within 6" of it when exiting. Units inside/outside transports may not target other units outside/inside transports, but may target the transport itself. When a transport is destroyed, units inside must take a dangerous terrain test, are Shaken, and must be placed fully within 6” of the transport before it’s removed.
3.6.0: May transport up to X friendly models or Heroes with up to Tough(6), and non-Heroes with up to Tough(3) which occupy 3 spaces each. Transports may deploy with units inside, and units may enter/exit by using any move action, getting within 2” to enter, and staying fully within 6” when exiting. Units inside/outside transports may not target other units that are outside/inside, but may target the transport itself. When a transport is destroyed, units inside must take a Dangerous Terrain Test, are Shaken, and must be placed fully within 6” of the transport before it’s removed.
✏️Unstoppable3.5.1: Ignores Regeneration, and ignores all negative modifiers to this weapon.
3.6.0: Ignores Regeneration, and ignores all negative modifiers to this weapon which are not from this model's special rules.
