Special rules

NameText
➕Enhanced FortifiedIf most models in this unit have Fortified, hits they take count as having AP(-2) from Fortified (instead of only AP(-1)).
➕Enhanced OssifiedIf most models in this unit have Ossified, hits they take count as having AP(-2) from Ossified (instead of only AP(-1)).
➕Enhanced Ossified AuraThis model's unit and up to 2 nearest friendly units picked before the start of the game get Enhanced Ossified. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
➕EntrenchedIf most models in this unit have this rule, as long as they haven't moved during this round, enemy units shooting at them from over 12” away get -1 to hit rolls.
➕FortifiedWhen units where most models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
➕ShatterFor each unmodified result of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+3).
➖Breath AttackOnce per activation, before attacking, roll one die. On a 2+ one enemy unit within 6" in line of sight takes 1 hit with Blast(3) and AP(1).
➖DestructiveOn unmodified results of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
➖Mobile ArtilleryWhen this model uses a Hold action and shoots at enemies over 9" away, it gets +1 to hit rolls. As long this model hasn't moved during the round, when enemy units shoot at it from over 9" away, they get -2 to hit rolls.
➖Ossified BoostIf all models in this unit have Ossified, enemy hits always count as having AP(-1) from Ossified (instead of only when being shot/charged from over 9" away).
➖Ossified Boost AuraThis model and up to 3 nearest friendly units picked before the start of the game get Ossified Boost. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
➖Piercing ShooterThis model gets AP(+1) when shooting.
➖PreciseGets +1 to hit when attacking.
➖ReinforcedWhen units where all models have this rule are shot or charged from over 9" away and take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
✏️Ambush
core rule		3.5.1: Only deploys D3 rounds afters its wave is deployed, and at the start of the round it's placed at the centre of a random table quarter.
3.6.0: Only deploys D3 rounds afters its wave, and at the start of the round it's placed within 6” of a random AI Goal.
✏️Bane in Melee Aura3.5.1: This model and up to 3 nearest friendly units picked before the start of the game get Bane in melee. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
3.6.0: This model's unit and up to 2 nearest friendly units picked before the start of the game get Bane in melee. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
✏️Evasive3.5.1: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls when attacking units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls when attacking units where most models have this rule.
✏️Evasive Aura3.5.1: This model and up to 3 nearest friendly units picked before the start of the game get Evasive. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
3.6.0: This model's unit and up to 2 nearest friendly units picked before the start of the game get Evasive. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
✏️Furious
core rule		3.5.1: When charging, unmodified results of 6 to hit in melee deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: When charging, unmodified result of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
