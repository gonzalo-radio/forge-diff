Special rules

NameText
➕Enhanced ChangeboundIf most models in this unit have Changebound, enemies attacking them always get -1 to hit rolls from Changebound (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕Enhanced ScreenedIf most models in this unit have Screened, enemies attacking them always get -1 to hit rolls from Screened (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕LacerateWhen attacking, the target must re-roll unmodified Defense results of 6.
➖Changebound BoostIf all models in this unit have Changebound, enemies attacking them always get -1 to hit rolls from Changebound (instead of only when being shot/charged from over 9" away).
➖PreciseGets +1 to hit when attacking.
➖SurgeOn unmodified results of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hits (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Bounding3.5.1: When this unit is activated, you may place all models with this rule in it anywhere fully within D3+1" of their position.
3.6.0: When this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3+1".
✏️Changebound3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot or charged from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: If most models in this unit have this rule, the first time they are attacked each round, enemy units attacking them get -1 to hit rolls.
✏️Fortified3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
3.6.0: When units where most models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
✏️Screened3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot or charged from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: If most models in this unit have this rule, the first time they are attacked each round, enemy units attacking them get -1 to hit rolls.
