➕Enhanced Royal WarriorIf this model has Royal Warrior, it gets extra attacks on successful unmodified hit results of 5-6 from Royal Warrior (instead of only on 6).
➕Enhanced Royal Warrior AuraThis model's unit and up to 2 nearest friendly units picked before the start of the game get Enhanced Royal Warrior. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
➕EntrenchedIf most models in this unit have this rule, as long as they haven't moved during this round, enemy units shooting at them from over 12” away get -1 to hit rolls.
➕Quick ThrowThis model may always target enemies that are not in line of sight when shooting with Indirect weapons (instead of only when using Hold actions).
➕Spell ResistanceWhen a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➖Breath AttackOnce per activation, before attacking, roll one die. On a 2+ one enemy unit within 6" in line of sight takes 1 hit with Blast(3) and AP(1).
➖Quick ReadjustmentThis model ignores penalties from shooting after moving when using Indirect weapons.

core rule		3.5.1: Only deploys D3 rounds afters its wave is deployed, and at the start of the round it's placed at the centre of a random table quarter.
3.6.0: Only deploys D3 rounds afters its wave, and at the start of the round it's placed within 6” of a random AI Goal.
✏️Indirect
core rule		3.5.1: Gets -1 to hit rolls when shooting after moving. May target enemies that are not in line of sight as if in line of sight, and ignores cover from sight obstructions.
3.6.0: When using Hold actions and shooting, may target enemies that are not in line of sight as if in line of sight, and ignores Cover from Sight Blockers.

3.6.0: Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Indirect against once (next time the effect would apply).

3.6.0: This model's unit and up to 2 nearest friendly units picked before the start of the game get Melee Slayer. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.

3.6.0: Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 nearest friendly units within 12", which gets +1 to hit rolls in melee once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.

3.6.0: Units where most models have this rule get +1 to Defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.

3.6.0: This model's unit and up to 2 nearest friendly units picked before the start of the game get Shielded. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
