Special rules

NameText
➕Crossing StrikeOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
➕Enhanced Angelic BlessingIf most models in this unit have Angelic Blessing, they always ignore wounds on rolls of 6+/2+ from Angelic Blessing (instead of only as long as they haven't started an activation yet).
➕Enhanced Angelic Blessing BuffOnce per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 friendly units within 12", which gets Enhanced Angelic Blessing once (next time the effect would apply).
➕Enhanced WardingIf most models in this unit have Warding, they always ignore wounds on rolls of 6+/2+ from Warding (instead of only as long as they haven't started an activation yet).
➕Piercing MarksmanWhen shooting at enemies over 9" away, this model's weapons get AP(+1).
