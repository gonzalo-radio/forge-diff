➖Precision TagOnce per game, during this model's activation, pick one enemy unit within 24" and in line of sight of it, and place X markers on it. When attacking, friendly units may remove markers from their target before rolling to hit to get +Y to hit rolls, where Y is the number of removed markers.

core rule		3.5.1: This model only rolls to check wound effects once it has taken at least X wounds, and is only Knocked Out on rolls of 5+X or more.
3.6.0: Once a model has taken as many wounds as its Tough value, it is killed (remove from play).
