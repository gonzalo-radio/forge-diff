Special rules

NameText
➕DodgeIf most models in this unit have this rule, as long as they aren't Fatigued, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls in melee against them.
➕Enhanced BrutalIf this model has Brutal, the target always takes hits on unmodified hit results of 6 from Brutal (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕Enhanced SavageIf this model has Savage, the target always takes hits on unmodified hit results of 6 from Savage (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕Enhanced Savage AuraThis model's unit and up to 2 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Enhanced Savage. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
➕InstinctiveWhen this model is activated, if it is able to shoot/charge an enemy unit, then it must immediately attack the closest valid target and gets +1 to hit rolls for that attack.
➕PierceIgnores Cover, and when attacking units over half of this weapon's range away, this weapon gets AP(+1).
➕Piercing FighterThis model gets AP(+1) in melee.
➖Savage BoostIf this model has Savage, when it shoots or charges enemies over 9" away, it deals extra hits on successful unmodified hit results of 5-6 from Savage (instead of only on 6).
➖Savage Boost AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Savage Boost. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
➖TearAgainst non-Hero units with Tough(3) or more, this weapon gets AP(+4).
✏️Blast
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X, where X is up to as many hits as enemy models in the target unit and within 3” of it. Hits must be split evenly between all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (defender picks how).
3.6.0: Ignores Cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X. Hits must be split evenly between the target and all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (defender picks how), but each unit may only take up to as many hits as models in it
✏️Bounding3.5.1: When this unit is activated, you may place all models with this rule in it anywhere fully within D3+1" of their position.
3.6.0: When this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3+1".
✏️Brutal3.5.1: When attacking, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: When this model attacks enemies that already finished an activation this round, unmodified result of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Counter-Attack3.5.1: Strikes first when charged.
3.6.0: May strike first when charged, unless this unit is Fatigued.
✏️Melee Evasion3.5.1: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where most models have this rule.
✏️Melee Evasion Aura3.5.1: This model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Melee Evasion. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
