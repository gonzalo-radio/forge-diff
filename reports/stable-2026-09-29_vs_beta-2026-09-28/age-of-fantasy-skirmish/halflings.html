Special rules

NameText
➕Crossing StrikeOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
➕DecimateWhen attacking units with more than half of their total size or Tough value (for single-model units), the target must re-roll unmodified Defense results of 6.
➕Enhanced BrutalIf this model has Brutal, the target always takes hits on unmodified hit results of 6 from Brutal (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕Enhanced LuckyIf this model has Lucky, the target always takes hits on unmodified hit results of 6 from Lucky (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕Enhanced Lucky AuraThis model's unit and up to 2 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Enhanced Lucky. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
➖Heavy ImpactCounts as having Impact(X) with hits that have AP(1).
➖LacerateWhen attacking the target must re-roll unmodified Defense results of 6.
➖Lucky BoostIf this model has Lucky, when it shoots or charges enemies over 9" away, it deals extra hits on successful unmodified hit results of 5-6 from Lucky (instead of only on 6).
➖Lucky Boost AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Lucky Boost. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
➖StrafingOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
✏️Blast
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X, where X is up to as many hits as enemy models in the target unit and within 3” of it. Hits must be split evenly between all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (defender picks how).
3.6.0: Ignores Cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X. Hits must be split evenly between the target and all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (defender picks how), but each unit may only take up to as many hits as models in it
✏️Bounding3.5.1: When this unit is activated, you may place all models with this rule in it anywhere fully within D3+1" of their position.
3.6.0: When this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3+1".
✏️Brutal3.5.1: When attacking, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: When this model attacks enemies that already finished an activation this round, unmodified result of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Fearless
core rule		3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule fails a morale test, roll one die. On a 4+ it counts as passed instead.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule fails a Morale Test, roll one die. On a 4+ it counts as passed instead.
✏️Lucky3.5.1: When attacking, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: When this model attacks enemies that already finished an activation this round, unmodified result of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Melee Evasion3.5.1: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where most models have this rule.
✏️Melee Slayer3.5.1: When this model charges, its weapons get AP(+2) if most models in the target have Tough(3) or higher.
3.6.0: When charging, this model's weapons get AP(+2) if most models in the target have Tough(9) or higher.
✏️Precision Fighter Aura3.5.1: This model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get +1 to hit rolls in melee. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
3.6.0: This model's unit and up to 2 friendly units picked before the start of the game get +1 to hit rolls in melee. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
✏️Regeneration
core rule		3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
