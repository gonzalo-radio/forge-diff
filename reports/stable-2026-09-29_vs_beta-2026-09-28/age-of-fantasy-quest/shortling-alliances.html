Special rules

NameText
➕Enhanced GuardedIf most models in this unit have Guarded, they always get +1 to Defense rolls from Guarded (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕Enhanced TenaciousIf most models in this unit have Tenacious, they always get +1 to Defense rolls from Tenacious (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕ObliterateIgnores Regeneration, and when attacking units where most models have Tough(27) or higher, the target must re-roll successful unmodified Defense results of 5-6.
➖ShredOn unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
➖Tenacious BoostIf all models in this unit have Tenacious, they always get +1 to defense rolls from Tenacious (instead of only when being shot/charged from over 9" away).
✏️Guarded3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot or charged from over 9" away, they get +1 to defense rolls.
3.6.0: If most models in this unit have this rule, the first time they are attacked each round, they get +1 to Defense rolls.
✏️Indirect when Shooting Aura3.5.1: This model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Indirect when shooting. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
3.6.0: This model's unit and up to 2 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Indirect when shooting. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule.
✏️Tenacious3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot or charged from over 9" away, they get +1 to defense rolls.
3.6.0: If most models in this unit have this rule, the first time they are attacked each round, they get +1 to Defense rolls.
