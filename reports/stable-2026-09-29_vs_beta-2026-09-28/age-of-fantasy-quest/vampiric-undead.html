Special rules

NameText
➕Enhanced Cursed UndeadIf most models in this unit have Cursed Undead, they ignore wounds on rolls of 5-6 from Cursed Undead (instead of only on 6+).
➕Enhanced ProtectedIf most models in this unit have Protected, they ignore wounds on rolls of 5-6 from Protected (instead of only on 6+).
➕FragmentWhen attacking units where most models have Defense 4+ to Defense 6+, the target must re-roll successful unmodified Defense results of 4-6.
➕Piercing MarksmanWhen shooting at enemies over 9" away, this model's weapons get AP(+1).
➕Spell ResistanceWhen a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➕Unique HeroThis hero may only be taken once per party.
➖Breath AttackOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, roll one die. On a 2+ one enemy unit within 6" in line of sight takes 1 hit with Blast(3) and AP(1).
➖Cursed Undead BoostIf all models in this unit have Cursed Undead, they ignore wounds on rolls of 5-6 from Cursed Undead (instead of only on 6+).
➖DisintegrateIgnores Regeneration, and against units where most models have Defense 2+ to Defense 3+, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➖Fast AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game moves +2" when using Advance actions and +4" when using Rush/Charge actions. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖Piercing HunterWhen this model shoots at enemies over 9" away, its weapons get AP(+1).
➖RavageWhen it's this model's turn to attack in melee, roll X dice. For each 6+ the target takes one wound.
➖ResistanceWhen a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➖SurgeOn unmodified results of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hits (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Caster
core rule		3.5.1: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t hold more than 6 tokens at once. At any point before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Roll one die, on 4+ resolve the effect on a target in line of sight. Heroes may spend any number of spell tokens before rolling to get +1 to the roll per token, or to give an enemy caster within 18” and in line of sight -1 to their roll per token. Whenever a hero fails to cast a spell, it may spend X*2 power to count as having cast it instead, where X is the spell’s casting value.
3.6.0: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t have more than 6 at once. Once per activation, before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Pick a target and roll one die, and on 4+ resolve the spell’s effect. Heroes may spend any number of tokens/power before rolling to get +1 to the roll per token/power, or to give an enemy Caster within 18” -1 to their roll per token/power.
✏️Cursed Undead3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored.
✏️Mind Control3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which must take a morale test. If failed you may move it by up to 6" in a straight line in any direction.
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which must take a Morale Test. If failed you may move it by up to 6" in a straight line in any direction. Units may only be moved once per round in this way.
✏️Protected3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored.
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Shred
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
