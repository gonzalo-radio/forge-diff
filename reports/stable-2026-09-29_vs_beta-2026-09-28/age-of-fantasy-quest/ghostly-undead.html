Special rules

NameText
➕Enhanced Empyrean SpiritIf most models in this unit have Empyrean Spirit, enemies attacking them always get -1 to hit rolls from Empyrean Spirit (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕Enhanced ScreenedIf most models in this unit have Screened, enemies attacking them always get -1 to hit rolls from Screened (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕Heavy ImpactCounts as having Impact(X) with hits that have AP(1).
➕ReapWhen attacking units that are Shaken or Fatigued, the target must re-roll successful unmodified Defense results of 5-6.
➕Spell ResistanceWhen a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➖DisintegrateIgnores Regeneration, and against units where most models have Defense 2+ to Defense 3+, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➖Empyrean Spirit BoostIf all models in this unit have Empyrean Spirit, enemies attacking them always get -1 to hit rolls from Empyrean Spirit (instead of only when being shot/charged from over 9" away).
➖ResistanceWhen a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
✏️Bane Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Bane when attacking against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Bane when attacking against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Empyrean Spirit3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot or charged from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: If most models in this unit have this rule, the first time they are attacked each round, enemy units attacking them get -1 to hit rolls.
✏️Ethereal3.5.1: Once per activation, before attacking, place this model anywhere fully within 6" of its position. This model moves -6" when using Advance, and -6” when using Rush/Charge.
3.6.0: Once per activation, before attacking, you may place this model anywhere fully within 6" of its position. If you do, this model moves -6" when using Advance, and -6" when using Rush/Charge until the end of that activation.
✏️Precision Feat3.5.1: Once per game, when this unit attacks and all its models have this rule, you may use this rule so that they get +1 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: Once per game, when this unit attacks, you may use this rule so that all models with this rule in it get +1 to hit rolls.
✏️Screened3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot or charged from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: If most models in this unit have this rule, the first time they are attacked each round, enemy units attacking them get -1 to hit rolls.
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Shielded3.5.1: Units where all models have this rule get +1 to defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.
3.6.0: Units where most models have this rule get +1 to Defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.
✏️Surge
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hits (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
