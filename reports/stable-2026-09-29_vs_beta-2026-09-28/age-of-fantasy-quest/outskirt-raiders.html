Special rules

NameText
➕Enhanced RaiderIf this model has Raider, enemies taking hits from it must re-roll successful unmodified Defense results of 5-6 from Raider (instead of only results of 6).
➕Enhanced ViciousIf this model has Vicious, enemies taking hits from it must re-roll successful unmodified Defense results of 5-6 from Vicious (instead of only results of 6).
➕Melee SlayerThis model's melee weapons get AP(+2) against units where most models have Tough(9) or higher.
➕PreciseGets +1 to hit when attacking.
➕Precision FighterThis model gets +1 to hit rolls in melee.
➕VengeanceWhen this Hero becomes unconscious, place one Vengeance marker on the unit that made it unconscious. Friendly units get +X to hit rolls with their weapons when attacking that unit, where X is the number of markers on it.
➖Precision Fighter AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get +1 to hit rolls in melee. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖Precision TagOnly one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, pick one enemy unit within 24" and in line of sight of it, and place X markers on it. When attacking, friendly units may remove markers from their target before rolling to hit to get +Y to hit rolls, where Y is the number of removed markers.
➖Raider BoostIf this model has Raider, when it shoots or charges enemies over 9" away, enemies taking hits from it must re-roll successful unmodified defense results of 5-6 from Raider (instead of only on 6).
➖Ranged SlayerWhen this model shoots at enemies over 9" away, its weapons get AP(+2) if most models in the target have Tough(3) or higher.
➖ShredOn unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
✏️Defense Debuff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to defense rolls once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to Defense rolls once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Shatter3.5.1: Against non-Hero units with Tough(3) or more, this weapon gets AP(+2).
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+3).
✏️Surge
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hits (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
