Special rules

NameText
➕Crossing StrikeOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
➕Enhanced Angelic BlessingIf most models in this unit have Angelic Blessing, they always ignore wounds on rolls of 6+/2+ from Angelic Blessing (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕Enhanced Angelic Blessing BuffOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 friendly units within 12", which gets Enhanced Angelic Blessing once (next time the effect would apply).
➕Enhanced WardingIf most models in this unit have Warding, they always ignore wounds on rolls of 6+/2+ from Warding (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕Piercing MarksmanWhen shooting at enemies over 9" away, this model's weapons get AP(+1).
➕ShatterFor each unmodified result of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+3).
➕Versatile WarriorWhen this unit is activated, pick one effect: until the end of the activation, when this unit attacks enemies for the first time each round, all models with this rule in it either get AP(+1) or get +1 to hit rolls.
➕Versatile Warrior BuffOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 friendly units within 12", which gets Versatile Warrior once (next time the effect would apply).
➖Angelic Blessing BoostIf all models in this unit have Angelic Blessing, they ignore wounds from spells on rolls of 2+ from Angelic Blessing (instead of only on 4+).
➖Angelic Blessing Boost BuffOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 friendly units within 12", which gets Angelic Blessing Boost once (next time the effect would apply).
➖Breath AttackOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, roll one die. On a 2+ one enemy unit within 6" in line of sight takes 1 hit with Blast(3) and AP(1).
➖Piercing HunterWhen this model shoots at enemies over 9" away, its weapons get AP(+1).
➖ShredOn unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
➖StrafingOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
➖TearAgainst non-Hero units with Tough(3) or more, this weapon gets AP(+4).
➖Versatile AttackWhen this unit is activated, pick one effect: until the end of the activation, when shooting or charging enemies over 9" away, all models with this rule in this unit either get AP(+1) or get +1 to hit rolls.
➖Versatile Attack BuffOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 friendly units within 12", which gets Versatile Attack once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Angelic Blessing3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 4+ instead.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, the first time they are attacked each round, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
✏️Caster
core rule		3.5.1: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t hold more than 6 tokens at once. At any point before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Roll one die, on 4+ resolve the effect on a target in line of sight. Heroes may spend any number of spell tokens before rolling to get +1 to the roll per token, or to give an enemy caster within 18” and in line of sight -1 to their roll per token. Whenever a hero fails to cast a spell, it may spend X*2 power to count as having cast it instead, where X is the spell’s casting value.
3.6.0: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t have more than 6 at once. Once per activation, before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Pick a target and roll one die, and on 4+ resolve the spell’s effect. Heroes may spend any number of tokens/power before rolling to get +1 to the roll per token/power, or to give an enemy Caster within 18” -1 to their roll per token/power.
✏️Flying
core rule		3.5.1: May move through units and terrain, and ignores terrain effects whilst moving, and automatically passes jumping rolls.
3.6.0: May move through units and terrain, and ignores the effects of terrain it doesn't end its move it. This unit automatically passes jumping rolls.
✏️Fortified3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
3.6.0: When units where most models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
✏️Precision Debuff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to hit rolls when attacking once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to hit rolls when attacking once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Slayer3.5.1: When this model shoots at enemies over 9" away, or when it charges, its weapons get AP(+2) if most models in the target have Tough(3) or higher.
3.6.0: When shooting enemies over 9" away, or when charging, this model's weapons get AP(+2) if most models in the target have Tough(27) or higher.
✏️Slayer Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Slayer against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Slayer against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Warding3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 4+ instead.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, the first time they are attacked each round, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
