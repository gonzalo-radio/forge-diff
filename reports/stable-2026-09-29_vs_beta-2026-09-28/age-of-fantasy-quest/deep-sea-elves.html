Special rules

NameText
➕Enhanced NimbleIf this model has Nimble, it may move by up to 2D3" from Nimble (instead of only D3").
➕Enhanced Wave-StepIf this model has Wave-Step, it may move by up to 2D3" from Wave-Step (instead of only D3").
➕ReapWhen attacking units that are Shaken or Fatigued, the target must re-roll successful unmodified Defense results of 5-6.
➖LacerateWhen attacking the target must re-roll unmodified Defense results of 6.
➖Wave-Step BoostIf this model has Wave-Step, it may be placed within 2D3" from Wave-Step (instead of only within D3").
✏️Blast
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X. Hits must be split evenly between the target and all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how), but each unit may only take up to as many hits as models in it.
3.6.0: Ignores cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X, where X is up to as many hits as enemy models in the target unit and within 3” of it. Hits must be split evenly between all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how).
✏️Caster
core rule		3.5.1: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t hold more than 6 tokens at once. At any point before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Roll one die, on 4+ resolve the effect on a target in line of sight. Heroes may spend any number of spell tokens before rolling to get +1 to the roll per token, or to give an enemy caster within 18” and in line of sight -1 to their roll per token. Whenever a hero fails to cast a spell, it may spend X*2 power to count as having cast it instead, where X is the spell’s casting value.
3.6.0: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t have more than 6 at once. Once per activation, before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Pick a target and roll one die, and on 4+ resolve the spell’s effect. Heroes may spend any number of tokens/power before rolling to get +1 to the roll per token/power, or to give an enemy Caster within 18” -1 to their roll per token/power.
✏️Nimble3.5.1: When this unit is activated, you may place all models with this rule in it anywhere fully within D3" of their position.
3.6.0: When this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3".
✏️Precision Debuff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to hit rolls when attacking once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to hit rolls when attacking once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Ranged Shrouding3.5.1: Enemies get -6" range to a min. of 6" when trying to shoot units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule is targeted by an enemy, as long as this unit hasn't started an activation yet this round, attackers get -4"" range to a min. of 6" when shooting at it.
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Shred
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
✏️Stealth
core rule		3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: When units where most models have this rule are shot from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
✏️Stealth Buff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 friendly units within 12", which gets Stealth once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 friendly units within 12", which gets Stealth once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Wave-Step3.5.1: When this unit is activated, you may place all models with this rule in it anywhere fully within D3" of their position.
3.6.0: When this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3".
