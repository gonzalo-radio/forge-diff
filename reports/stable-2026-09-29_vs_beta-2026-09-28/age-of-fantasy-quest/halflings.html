Special rules

NameText
➕DecimateWhen attacking units with more than half of their total size or Tough value (for single-model units), the target must re-roll unmodified Defense results of 6.
➕Enhanced BrutalIf this model has Brutal, the target always takes hits on unmodified hit results of 6 from Brutal (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕Enhanced LuckyIf this model has Lucky, the target always takes hits on unmodified hit results of 6 from Lucky (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕Precision FighterThis model gets +1 to hit rolls in melee.
➖Heavy ImpactCounts as having Impact(X) with hits that have AP(1).
➖LacerateWhen attacking the target must re-roll unmodified Defense results of 6.
➖Lucky BoostIf this model has Lucky, when it shoots or charges enemies over 9" away, it deals extra hits on successful unmodified hit results of 5-6 from Lucky (instead of only on 6).
➖Precision Fighter AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get +1 to hit rolls in melee. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
✏️Blast
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X. Hits must be split evenly between the target and all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how), but each unit may only take up to as many hits as models in it.
3.6.0: Ignores cover, and after resolving other special rules, each hit is multiplied by X, where X is up to as many hits as enemy models in the target unit and within 3” of it. Hits must be split evenly between all enemy units within 3” of any model from the target (players pick how).
✏️Brutal3.5.1: When attacking, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: When this model attacks enemies for the first time each round, unmodified result of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Fearless
core rule		3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule fails a morale test, roll one die. On a 4+ it counts as passed instead.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule fails a Morale Test, roll one die. On a 4+ it counts as passed instead.
✏️Lucky3.5.1: When attacking, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: When this model attacks enemies for the first time each round, unmodified result of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Melee Evasion3.5.1: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where most models have this rule.
✏️Morale Debuff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to morale test rolls once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to Morale Test rolls once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Rending
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Regeneration, and on unmodified results of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
3.6.0: Ignores Regeneration, and for each unmodified result of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Takedown
core rule		3.5.1: This model may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target, which is resolved as if it was a unit of [1]. Takedown attacks must be resolved before other weapons.
3.6.0: This model may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target for this weapon's attacks, which is resolved as if it was a unit of [1]. Takedown attacks must be resolved before other weapons.
✏️Takedown Strike3.5.1: Once per game, when it's this model's turn to attack in melee, it may make one attack at Quality 2+ with AP(2), Deadly(3), and Takedown.
3.6.0: Once per game, when it's this model's turn to strike in melee, it may make X attacks at Quality 2+ with AP(2), Deadly(3), and Takedown.
