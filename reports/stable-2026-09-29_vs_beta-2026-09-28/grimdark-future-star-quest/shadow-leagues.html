Special rules

NameText
➕Ambush Re-DeploymentOnce per game, when a unit where all models have this rule ends its activation, you may immediately remove it from the table (dropping any objectives it might hold within 1"), and deploy it as if it had Ambush at the beginning of the next round.
➕Enhanced Shadow OperativeIf most models in this unit have Shadow Operative, attackers always get -4"/-2" range when targeting it from Shadow Operative (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕Enhanced ShroudedIf most models in this unit have Shrouded, attackers always get -4"/-2" range when targeting it from Shrouded (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕PurgeWhen attacking units that are Shaken or Fatigued, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➖DisintegrateIgnores Regeneration, and against units where most models have Defense 2+ to Defense 3+, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➖Precision TargetOnly one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, pick one enemy unit within 18" and in line of sight of it, and place X markers on it. Friendly units get +X to hit rolls when attacking it.
➖Shadow Operative BoostIf all models in this unit have Shadow Operative, enemies get -8" range to a min. of 6" when shooting them, and -4" movement to a min. of 6" when charging them from Shadow Operative (instead of only -4" and -2").
✏️Precision Debuff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to hit rolls when attacking once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to hit rolls when attacking once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Shadow Operative3.5.1: Enemies get -4" range to a min. of 6" when trying to shoot at units where all models have this rule, and -2" movement to a min. of 6" when trying to charge units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule is targeted by an enemy, as long as this unit hasn't started an activation yet this round, attackers get -4"/-2" range to a min. of 6" when shooting/charging it.
✏️Shred
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
✏️Shrouded3.5.1: Enemies get -4" range to a min. of 6" when trying to shoot at units where all models have this rule, and -2" movement to a min. of 6" when trying to charge units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule is targeted by an enemy, as long as this unit hasn't started an activation yet this round, attackers get -4"/-2" range to a min. of 6" when shooting/charging it.
✏️Takedown
core rule		3.5.1: This model may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target, which is resolved as if it was a unit of [1]. Takedown attacks must be resolved before other weapons.
3.6.0: This model may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target for this weapon's attacks, which is resolved as if it was a unit of [1]. Takedown attacks must be resolved before other weapons.
✏️Takedown Strike3.5.1: Once per game, when it's this model's turn to attack in melee, it may make one attack at Quality 2+ with AP(2), Deadly(3), and Takedown.
3.6.0: Once per game, when it's this model's turn to strike in melee, it may make X attacks at Quality 2+ with AP(2), Deadly(3), and Takedown.
