➖DisintegrateIgnores Regeneration, and against units where most models have Defense 2+ to Defense 3+, this weapon gets AP(+2).

➖Primal Boost BuffOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 friendly units within 12", which gets Primal Boost once (next time the effect would apply).
➖RavageWhen it's this model's turn to attack in melee, roll X dice. For each 6+ the target takes one wound.
➖ShieldedUnits where all models have this rule get +1 to defense rolls against hits that are not from spells.

3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Bane when attacking against once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Bounding3.5.1: When this unit is activated, you may place all models with this rule in it anywhere fully within D3+1" of their position.
3.6.0: When this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3+1".
✏️Caster
core rule		3.5.1: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t hold more than 6 tokens at once. At any point before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Roll one die, on 4+ resolve the effect on a target in line of sight. Heroes may spend any number of spell tokens before rolling to get +1 to the roll per token, or to give an enemy caster within 18” and in line of sight -1 to their roll per token. Whenever a hero fails to cast a spell, it may spend X*2 power to count as having cast it instead, where X is the spell’s casting value.
3.6.0: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t have more than 6 at once. Once per activation, before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Pick a target and roll one die, and on 4+ resolve the spell’s effect. Heroes may spend any number of tokens/power before rolling to get +1 to the roll per token/power, or to give an enemy Caster within 18” -1 to their roll per token/power.
✏️Evasive3.5.1: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls when attacking units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls when attacking units where most models have this rule.
✏️Fortified3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
3.6.0: When units where most models have this rule take hits, those hits count as having AP(-1), to a min. of AP(0).
✏️Furious
core rule		3.5.1: When charging, unmodified results of 6 to hit in melee deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: When charging, unmodified result of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Impact
core rule		3.5.1: Roll X dice when attacking after charging, unless fatigued. For each 2+ the target takes one hit.
3.6.0: When charging, before either unit attacks, roll X dice, unless this unit is Fatigued. For each 2+ the target takes 1 hit.
✏️Precision Target3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, pick one enemy unit within 18" and in line of sight of it, and place X markers on it. Friendly units get +X to hit rolls when attacking it.
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, pick X enemy units within 18" and in line of sight of it, and place one token on them. Friendly units get +1 to hit rolls when attacking them.
✏️Protection Feat3.5.1: Once per game, when this unit takes wounds and all its models have this rule, you may use this rule and roll one die per wound, and on a 5+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: Once per game, when this unit takes wounds and most of its models have this rule, you may use this rule and roll one die per wound, and on a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Regeneration
core rule		3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 5+ it is ignored.
✏️Rending
core rule		3.5.1: Ignores Regeneration, and on unmodified results of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
3.6.0: Ignores Regeneration, and for each unmodified result of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
✏️Shred
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
