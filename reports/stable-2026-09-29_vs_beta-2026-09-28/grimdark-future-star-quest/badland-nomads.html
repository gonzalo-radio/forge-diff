Special rules

NameText
➕DisintegrateIgnores Regeneration, and when attacking units where most models have Tough(27) or higher, this weapon gets AP(+2).
➕Enhanced Badlands HunterIf this model has Badlands Hunter, enemies always take wounds on unmodified results of 1 to block from Badlands Hunter (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕Enhanced ViolentIf this model has Violent, enemies always take wounds on unmodified results of 1 to block from Violent (instead of only when attacking enemies that already finished an activation).
➕Precision FighterThis model gets +1 to hit rolls in melee.
➕VengeanceWhen this Hero becomes unconscious, place one Vengeance marker on the unit that made it unconscious. Friendly units get +X to hit rolls with their weapons when attacking that unit, where X is the number of markers on it.
➖Badlands Hunter BoostIf this model has Badlands Hunter, when it shoots or charges enemies over 9" away, enemies taking wounds from it take extra wounds on failed defense rolls of 1-2 from Badlands Hunter (instead of only on 1).
➖Precision Fighter AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get +1 to hit rolls in melee. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖Precision TagOnly one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, pick one enemy unit within 24" and in line of sight of it, and place X markers on it. When attacking, friendly units may remove markers from their target before rolling to hit to get +Y to hit rolls, where Y is the number of removed markers.
➖TearAgainst non-Hero units with Tough(3) or more, this weapon gets AP(+4).
✏️Badlands Hunter3.5.1: Enemies that roll to block hits from this model's weapons take 1 extra wound for each unmodified result of 1 that they roll.
3.6.0: When this model attacks enemies for the first time each round, unmodified results of 1 to block hits from weapons deal 1 extra wound.
✏️Bounding3.5.1: When this unit is activated, you may place all models with this rule in it anywhere fully within D3+1" of their position.
3.6.0: When this unit is activated, you may move all models with this rule in it by up to D3+1".
✏️Defense Debuff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to defense rolls once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to Defense rolls once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Violent3.5.1: Enemies that roll to block hits from this model's weapons take 1 extra wound for each unmodified result of 1 that they roll.
3.6.0: When this model attacks enemies for the first time each round, unmodified results of 1 to block hits from weapons deal 1 extra wound.
