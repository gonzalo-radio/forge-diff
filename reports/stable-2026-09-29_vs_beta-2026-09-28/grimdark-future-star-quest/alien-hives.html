Special rules

NameText
➕Crossing AttackOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and roll X dice. For each 6+ the target takes one wound.
➕Crossing StrikeOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
➕Enhanced Hive BondIf most models in this unit have Hive Bond, they always get +1 to Morale Test rolls from Hive Bond (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕Enhanced ValiantIf most models in this unit have Valiant, they always get +1 to Morale Test rolls from Valiant (instead of only the first time they are attacked each round).
➕FragmentWhen attacking units where most models have Defense 4+ to Defense 6+, the target must re-roll successful unmodified Defense results of 4-6.
➕Furious BuffOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 friendly units within 12", which gets Furious once (next time the effect would apply).
➕ImpreciseGets -1 to hit when attacking.
➕Piercing TargetOnly one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, pick X enemy units within 18" and in line of sight of it, and place one token on them. Friendly units get AP(+1) on their weapons when attacking them.
➕PredatorFor each unmodified roll of 6 to hit when attacking, this model may roll +1 attack with that weapon. This rule doesn’t apply to newly generated attacks.
➕Spell ResistanceWhen a unit where most models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➕Unique HeroThis hero may only be taken once per party.
➕UnwieldyStrikes last when charging.
➕ValiantIf most models in this unit have this rule, the first time they are attacked each round, they get +1 to Morale Test rolls.
➖Breath AttackOnly one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, roll one die. On a 2+ one enemy unit within 6" in line of sight takes 1 hit with Blast(3) and AP(1).
➖CourageousUnits where all models have this rule get +1 to morale test rolls.
➖DestructiveOn unmodified results of 6 to hit, those hits get AP(+4).
➖Furious AuraThis model and up to 3 friendly units picked before the start of the game get Furious. If this model is killed, picked models that are deployed lose the rule. This effect lasts until the first wave of reinforcements is deployed.
➖Hive Bond BoostIf all models in this unit have Hive Bond, they get +2 to morale test rolls from Hive Bond (instead of only +1).
➖Piercing TagOnly one use per game. Once per game, during this model's activation, pick one enemy unit within 24" and in line of sight of it, and place X markers on it. When attacking, friendly units may remove markers from their target before rolling to block to get +AP(Y) on their weapons, where Y is the number of removed markers.
➖Predator FighterFor each unmodified roll of 6 to hit in melee, this model may roll +1 attack with that weapon. This rule doesn’t apply to newly generated attacks.
➖ResistanceWhen a unit where all models have this rule takes wounds, roll one die for each. On a 6+ it is ignored. If the wounds were from a spell, then they are ignored on a 2+ instead.
➖ShredOn unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
➖StrafingOnce per activation, when this model moves through enemy units, pick one of them and attack it with this weapon as if it was shooting. This weapon may only be used in this way.
➖SurgeOn unmodified results of 6 to hit, this weapon deals 1 extra hits (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Caster
core rule		3.5.1: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t hold more than 6 tokens at once. At any point before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Roll one die, on 4+ resolve the effect on a target in line of sight. Heroes may spend any number of spell tokens before rolling to get +1 to the roll per token, or to give an enemy caster within 18” and in line of sight -1 to their roll per token. Whenever a hero fails to cast a spell, it may spend X*2 power to count as having cast it instead, where X is the spell’s casting value.
3.6.0: Gets X spell tokens at the start of each round, but can’t have more than 6 at once. Once per activation, before attacking, spend as many tokens as the spell’s value to try casting one or more spells (only one try per spell). Pick a target and roll one die, and on 4+ resolve the spell’s effect. Heroes may spend any number of tokens/power before rolling to get +1 to the roll per token/power, or to give an enemy Caster within 18” -1 to their roll per token/power.
✏️Furious
core rule		3.5.1: When charging, unmodified results of 6 to hit in melee deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: When charging, unmodified result of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
✏️Hit & Run Fighter3.5.1: Once per round, units where all models have this rule may move by up to 3" after being in melee.
3.6.0: Once per round, this model may move by up to 3" after being in melee.
✏️Hive Bond3.5.1: Units where all models have this rule get +1 to morale test rolls.
3.6.0: If most models in this unit have this rule, the first time they are attacked each round, they get +1 to Morale Test rolls.
✏️Precision Debuff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to hit rolls when attacking once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which gets -1 to hit rolls when attacking once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Regenerative Strength3.5.1: Place one marker on this model when it ignores a wound. When in melee, pick one of its weapons to get +X attacks, where X is the number of markers on it.
3.6.0: Place one token on this model when it ignores a wound. Once per round, when it’s this model’s turn to strike in melee, roll as many dice as tokens on it before attacking, unless this unit is Fatigued. For each 4+ the target takes one wound.
✏️Stealth
core rule		3.5.1: When units where all models have this rule are shot from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
3.6.0: When units where most models have this rule are shot from over 9" away, enemy units get -1 to hit rolls.
✏️Stealth Buff3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 friendly units within 12", which gets Stealth once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 friendly units within 12", which gets Stealth once (next time the effect would apply).
✏️Takedown
core rule		3.5.1: This model may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target, which is resolved as if it was a unit of [1]. Takedown attacks must be resolved before other weapons.
3.6.0: This model may pick any model in the target unit as its individual target for this weapon's attacks, which is resolved as if it was a unit of [1]. Takedown attacks must be resolved before other weapons.
✏️Takedown Strike3.5.1: Once per game, when it's this model's turn to attack in melee, it may make one attack at Quality 2+ with AP(2), Deadly(3), and Takedown.
3.6.0: Once per game, when it's this model's turn to strike in melee, it may make X attacks at Quality 2+ with AP(2), Deadly(3), and Takedown.
✏️Unpredictable Fighter Mark3.5.1: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 4 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Unpredictable Fighter against once (next time the effect would apply).
3.6.0: Only one use per game. Once per activation, before attacking, pick up to 3 enemy units within 18" in line of sight, which friendly units gets Unpredictable Fighter against once (next time the effect would apply).
