➖Warbound BoostIf this model has Warbound, when it shoots or charges enemies over 9" away, enemies taking wounds from it take extra wounds on failed defense rolls of 1-2 from Warbound (instead of only on 1).

3.6.0: Enemies get -1 to hit rolls in melee when attacking units where most models have this rule.
✏️Ranged Shrouding3.5.1: Enemies get -6" range to a min. of 6" when trying to shoot units where all models have this rule.
3.6.0: When a unit where most models have this rule is targeted by an enemy, as long as this unit hasn't started an activation yet this round, attackers get -4"" range to a min. of 6" when shooting at it.

core rule		3.5.1: May be set aside before deployment. After all other units are deployed, may be deployed anywhere fully within 12” of their deployment zone. Players alternate in placing Scout units, starting with the player that activates next.
3.6.0: May be set aside before deployment. After all other units are deployed, may be deployed anywhere fully within 9” of their deployment zone, over 9” away from non-Scout enemy units. Players alternate in placing Scout units, starting with the player that activates next.
✏️Sergeant
core rule		3.5.1: When this model attacks, unmodified results of 6 to hit deal 1 extra hit (only the original hit counts as a 6 for special rules).
3.6.0: This model gets AP(+1) when attacking.
✏️Shred
becomes a core rule		3.5.1: On unmodified results of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
3.6.0: For each unmodified result of 1 to block hits, this weapon deals 1 extra wound.
✏️Steadfast3.5.1: If a unit where all models have this rule is Shaken at the beginning of the round, roll one die. On a 4+, it stops being Shaken.
3.6.0: If a unit where most models have this rule is Shaken at the beginning of the round, roll one die. On a 4+, it stops being Shaken.
✏️Vanguard3.5.1: After this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9” of its position.
3.6.0: After this model is deployed, it may be placed anywhere fully within 9" of its position, over 9" away from non-Vanguard enemy units.
✏️Versatile Defense3.5.1: When a unit where all models have this rule is deployed or activated, pick one effect: when shot or charged from over 9" away, the unit either gets +1 to defense rolls, or enemy units get -1 to hit rolls against it. This effect lasts until the units' next activation.
3.6.0: As long as a unit where most models have this rule hasn't activated yet this round, pick one effect when it is attacked: the unit either gets +1 to Defense rolls, or enemy units get -1 to hit rolls against it. Note that you may pick a different effect each time the unit is attacked.
✏️Violent3.5.1: Enemies that roll to block hits from this model's weapons take 1 extra wound for each unmodified result of 1 that they roll.
3.6.0: When this model attacks enemies that already finished an activation this round, unmodified results of 1 to block hits from weapons deal 1 extra wound.
✏️Warbound3.5.1: Enemies that roll to block hits from this model's weapons take 1 extra wound for each unmodified result of 1 that they roll.
3.6.0: When this model attacks enemies that already finished an activation this round, unmodified results of 1 to block hits from weapons deal 1 extra wound.
